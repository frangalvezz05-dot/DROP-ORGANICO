## Why it works for your week

**Not the same lunch five days in a row.** Three bases, twelve sauces. One batch of chicken can be Mexican on Monday and Mediterranean on Wednesday.

**One sheet, not a pile of recipes.** The Sunday sheet puts everything in order, with minutes and amounts per meal, so you cook without rereading or guessing. The plan is built around about 2 hours for one person's week. That is a target, not a promise: your first Sunday will probably take longer.

**It lasts, or it tells you when it won't.** Every recipe shows how long it keeps in the fridge and in the freezer. Anything that won't last until the end of the week goes into the freezer on Sunday.

**You know the cost and the protein before you shop.** Cost per plate, calories and protein are on every plate. The portions add up, so you don't buy for four when you cook for one.

## Made for you if…
- You've tried meal prep and got tired of the same food, or lost a Sunday to confusing steps
- You work full time and want your week planned in one sitting
- You cook for 1 or 2 people
- You want food that tastes like normal food, with the numbers already done
- You are a beginner or an average cook. Nothing here needs special skills

## FAQ

**When and how do I get it?**
Right after payment, by email, with a link to download the PDFs. Nothing is shipped.

**Do I need a special app?**
No. Any PDF reader works: the one on your phone, tablet or computer, or your web browser.

**Can I print it?**
Yes. The printable pack comes in US Letter and A4 and is designed to print in black and white. The ebook is made to be read on screen.

**Is it for one person or two?**
Both. The shopping lists and amounts are written for 1 and for 2 people.

**How long does the food last?**
Each recipe shows its fridge and freezer days, following USDA food-safety guidance. Cooked food usually keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge. The book gives you a simple rule for what to freeze on Sunday so nothing is left over on Friday.

**Does the 2 hours include everything?**
The plan is designed around about 2 hours of cooking for one person's week. It is a target, not a guarantee. Your first Sunday will likely take longer, and cooking for two adds cutting and portioning time.

**Do I need special equipment?**
Mostly an oven, a stovetop and a few sheet pans. A slow cooker appears in one week, and the recipe gives an oven option. The pantry and gear page lists everything.

**What about allergens?**
Each recipe and each plate marks the main allergens: milk, egg, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy and sesame. Always read the labels of what you buy.

**Are the nutrition numbers exact?**
They are estimates calculated from USDA FoodData Central. They can differ from your ingredients and brands.

**Are the costs exact?**
No. They are estimates from average US retail prices on the dates shown, and they change by store and city. The book also shows you how to work out your own.

**Will this help me lose weight?**
It's a cooking and planning guide. It isn't a diet or a treatment plan, and we don't promise any result with weight, health, savings or time.

**Is this a subscription?**
No. One payment of $24.99 USD.

**Where do you sell?**
This store sells to customers in the United States and the European Union.
