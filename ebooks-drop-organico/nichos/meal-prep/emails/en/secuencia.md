- **Subject:** Keep the sauce out of the container
- **Preheader:** A small change that keeps Wednesday's lunch tasting like lunch.
- **CTA:** reply to this email (tell us which sauce you reached for first)
- **Angles:** A2 + A4. Real situation: tired of the same food / throwing food away (avatar fears 1 and 3). Facts come from the storage table of the book.

**FINAL TEXT**

Hi,

If you've started on The Mini Sunday, here is one mistake that is very easy to make: stirring the sauce into the rice and chicken on Sunday.

By Wednesday the rice has soaked it all up, and every container tastes the same. That is how the same meal comes back five days in a row.

What works better:
- **Pack the base and protein in one container, the sauce in a small lidded cup.** Add it on the day.
- **Each sauce behaves differently.** The chipotle-lime yogurt is served cold and isn't heated; stir it first. The teriyaki is warmed for about 30 seconds and thickens as it cools. The lemon-tahini sets firm in the fridge, so whisk in 1 tablespoon of water per portion.
- **Reheat the chicken and rice covered, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).**

One Sunday batch, three sauces, three different plates. That's the whole idea.

**Just reply and tell us which sauce you reached for first.**
