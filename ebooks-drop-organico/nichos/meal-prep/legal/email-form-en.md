## Double opt-in (recommended)
After you submit the form you get an email: "Confirm your email to receive the sample" with a **Confirm** button. When you click it, we send the sample and store the date, time and accepted text. If you ticked box 2, the marketing consent is recorded only after that confirmation.

## Unsubscribe
Every marketing email has a **one-click unsubscribe** link, with no login and no explanation needed, and a valid contact address ({{EMAIL_CONTACTO}}).
