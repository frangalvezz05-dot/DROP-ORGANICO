# Cookie policy

## What they are

## Types we may use
- **Technical (necessary):** make the store work (cart, secure checkout, language, security). They do not need your consent.

- **Advertising or ad measurement:** only active if you accept them.

## Your choice
When you arrive you will see a notice with **Accept** and **Reject** buttons of the same size and prominence. Nothing is pre-ticked: if you do not choose, only technical cookies are used. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, as easily as you gave it, from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
