Looking for the plate with the most protein? One without milk? This index lists all 48 signature plates, sorted by protein, highest first. Then it lists which plates are free of each allergen.

## How the numbers were made

- One box = one standard portion of each of the 4 parts (protein, base, vegetable, sauce), as on the recipe cards.
- Calculated from **USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy** with the gram weight of every ingredient, sauce included. Kcal rounded to 10, protein, carbs and fat to 1 g.
- Your boxes in the plan weeks can differ, because some batches are shared by more or fewer boxes. The week chapters show those numbers.
- These numbers describe the food. They are not advice on how much to eat. For that, talk to a registered dietitian or your doctor.

**At a glance:** plates run from 510 to 850 kcal. 42 of 48 have 40 g of protein or more. The 6 under 40 g are the beef-and-lentil plates (M4, D-6, C5) and the chickpea plates (M7, D-2, D-9). Their real number is shown, not rounded up.
