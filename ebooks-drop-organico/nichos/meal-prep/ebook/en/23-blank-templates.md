After Week 4, the book has done its job if you can plan a week without it. These templates are the tools for that. They're in the printable pack (PDF, Letter and A4, works in black and white). Print a stack, or fill them on a tablet.

## What's in the printable pack, and when you use each page

| Template | Use it when | What you fill in |
|---|---|---|
| **Blank Sunday Sheet** | Saturday night, planning your own week | Header (week, ×1/×2, oven temps). Timeline rows: clock, station (OVEN / STOVE / COLD / ASSEMBLY), task, done-when, recipe. Assembly table: part, box count, grams per box. Friday box. Real time. |
| **Blank shopping list by aisle** | Before you shop | Meat & fish · Produce · Dairy & eggs · Bakery · Pantry & canned · Condiments · Spice check. Two columns: ×1 and ×2. A "leftover from last week" box at the top so you don't rebuy it. |
| **Build Your Plate** | Trying a combo that isn't one of the 48 | 4 rows (protein, base, vegetable, sauce) × kcal, protein, carbs, fat, allergens, fridge days. A total row. A check: does it pass the Mix & Match rule? |
| **Cost per box** | Once a month, or when prices jump | Item, package price, package size, amount used, cost used. Totals per week and per box. Store and date. |
| **Storage chart** | Every day, on the cupboard door | The 60 recipes from Chapter 20 on 2 pages. |
| **Mix & Match poster** | Planning | The four ✓ tables from Chapter 16 on one page. |
| **Pantry checklist** | Once, then each month | The basics from Chapter 7 with a tick box. |
| **Box labels** | Sunday, while you pack | Mon-Fri "eat by" labels, plate ID, cook date, a ❄ box for frozen ones. |
| **Fridge cards** | On the fridge door | The day-5 rule and Thursday reminder · reheating by part · the 12 sauces at a glance. |

The four filled Sunday Sheets and shopping lists from Weeks 1-4 are in the pack too, one page each, so you can repeat a week you liked without this book open.

## How to fill a blank Sunday Sheet (5 steps)

1. **Write your parts.** 2 proteins, 2 bases, 2 vegetables, 3 sauces, 1 breakfast. Check them on the Mix & Match chart.
2. **List the oven items by temperature, hottest first.** Each recipe card has a "Sunday-sheet slot" line with its temperature and minutes. Copy it.
3. **Fill the gaps with stove and cold tasks.** Anything marked "Stovetop" or "Cold" goes in the ⏱ rows while the oven runs.
4. **Assembly table.** For each part, count how many boxes use it. Weigh the batch on Sunday and divide.
5. **Friday.** Choose the plate whose hot parts all freeze (storage chart). Write it in the freezer row.
