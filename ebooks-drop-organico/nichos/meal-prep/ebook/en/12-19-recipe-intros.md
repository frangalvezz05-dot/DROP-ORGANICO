> [SIN DATO en EN: no hay frase real en inglés sobre bases que saben mal al recalentar. La frase original es de un foro español (E3) y no se traduce ni se presenta como real aquí.]

Rice, quinoa, potatoes, sweet potatoes, pasta and tortillas. The base is the part that usually ruins day 4: hard rice, mushy pasta, soggy potatoes. Each card is written to stop that.

**How to use this block**
- **Rice and quinoa:** cool them fast and spread thin (Chapter 4). Reheat with 1 Tbsp of water, covered.
- **Pasta:** cook it 1-2 minutes short, toss with oil. It finishes cooking when you reheat it.
- **Potatoes:** reheat in the oven or air fryer. The microwave makes them soft. Potatoes don't freeze well; sweet potatoes do.
- **Tortillas:** no cooking. Store them in a bag and roll the wrap at lunch, so it isn't soggy.

# 14 · Vegetables

> [SIN DATO: no hay frase real del avatar sobre verdura blanda. Técnica estándar.]

Four roasted, two raw. Roasted vegetables cook hot and fast so they don't turn to mush by Thursday. Raw ones are cut and dressed separately so they stay crisp.

**How to use this block**
- **Space on the pan is the whole trick.** One layer, pieces not touching. Crowded vegetables steam and go limp. Use a second pan.
- **Broccoli roasts hottest** (450 °F). Do it first on Sunday, then lower the oven.
- **Zucchini (V3) and the cucumber-tomato salad (V6) keep 3 days, not 4.** Plan them for Monday-Wednesday.
- **Dressings stay apart** until you eat (V5, V6).
- Most vegetables don't freeze well. The fajita peppers (V2) are the exception.

# 15 · Sauces · The Variety Engine

> "I noticed you reuse a lot of ingredients, seasonings, and sauces like the hot honey."
> — reader review

<!-- Frase F11 (frases.md), fragmento literal. Reseña de comprador en Gumroad (PrepWithDrew): https://prepwithdrew.gumroad.com/l/mealplan . Apoyo: F24 (Goodreads, Cook Once) -->

Same chicken, three sauces, three different meals. That's how one Sunday gives you five plates that don't taste like the same box. There are 12 sauces, 3 per flavor family. Most take 5-15 minutes and one small saucepan, or no heat at all.

**How to use this block**
- **Pick 3 sauces a week**, ideally from 2-3 families.
- **Store each sauce in its own cup.** It goes on after reheating. Sauce stored on rice or pasta makes it soggy.
- **Yogurt sauces (S1, S7, S11) are never heated** and don't freeze. Everything else freezes for 2-3 months: make a double batch and freeze half flat in a bag.
- Sauces are where the allergens hide: soy sauce (soy, usually wheat), tahini (sesame), peanut butter (peanuts), Worcestershire (fish), yogurt (milk). Check the column.

# 18 · The Freezer Block · 10 One-Pot Meals

> "Keep em in the freezer and you're good for weeks."
> — reader review

<!-- Frase F1 (frases.md), fragmento literal. Reseña de comprador en Gumroad (The Big Bean): https://soberfitness.gumroad.com/l/thebigbean -->

For the weeks when Sunday doesn't happen. Each recipe is one pot or one dish, makes 6 portions, and is built to freeze. Cook one on any free evening, portion it, freeze it, and take out one box at a time.

**How to use this block**
- **This is Path C** from Chapter 0. You can live on this block alone for a while.
- **Freeze on the day you cook.** Not on day 3 (freezing doesn't reset the clock).
- **Shallow containers**, 2 inches (5 cm) deep or less, so they cool fast and thaw evenly.
- **Thaw in the fridge overnight, never on the counter.** Reheat until 165 °F / 74 °C in the center.
- **Label every box** with the name and date. Quality is best within the months in the table.
