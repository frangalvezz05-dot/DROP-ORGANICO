### D8 · Turkey Sausage & Sweet Potato Hash
**Makes:** 5 portions (1 heaping cup, about 230 g) · **Active:** 20 min · **Total:** 50 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 310 kcal · 21 g protein · 30 g carbs · 13 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $1.70 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Skillet over medium heat 3-4 min, or microwave 90 sec, stirring once, until steaming and 165 °F / 74 °C.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Top with a fried or poached egg the morning you eat it (not counted).

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1 lb ground turkey (93% lean) (454 g)
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled, 1/2-inch cubes (600 g)
- 1 large yellow onion, diced (150 g)
- 1 large red bell pepper, diced (150 g)
- 2 Tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika (4.6 g)
- 1 tsp dried thyme (1 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp fine salt (9 g)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C with two rimmed sheet pans inside.
2. Toss the sweet potato, onion and pepper with the oil, paprika, thyme, garlic powder, salt and pepper in a big bowl.
3. Spread over the two hot pans in one layer with space between. Roast 15 min until the potato edges just start to brown.
4. Pull the pans out. Crumble the raw turkey in small clumps over the vegetables, then stir roughly.
5. Roast 15-18 min more, stirring once at the halfway point, until the turkey has no pink, the potato is soft in the middle and crisp at the edges, and a thermometer in the turkey reads 165 °F / 74 °C.
6. Cool 15 min on the pans. Mix well, divide into 5 containers (about 1 heaping cup each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, 35 min at 425 °F. Use the oven slot when the proteins and veg are not full; fits with P1 or V1 at the same temperature.

**Fix it:**
- Soft, not crisp? The pans were crowded. Use two pans and don't stack the vegetables.
- Dry turkey? It stayed in too long. Break the clumps smaller next time so it cooks faster.

**Swap:** Cheaper: use regular ground pork or beef (reheat to 160 °F / 71 °C; macros change). More veg: add 2 cups chopped kale in the last 5 min.
