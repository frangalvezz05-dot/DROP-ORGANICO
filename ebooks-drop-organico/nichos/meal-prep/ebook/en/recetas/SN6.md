### SN6 · Turkey & Cheese Roll-Ups
**Makes:** 5 portions (3 roll-ups (about 135 g)) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 15 min
**Per portion:** 210 kcal · 22 g protein · 4 g carbs · 11 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Cost/portion:** about $1.90 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (see Reheat) · **Reheat:** Eat cold. Do not freeze (cheese and cucumber go grainy and watery).
**Goes with:** B6 Wraps & Tortillas (roll a few inside a wrap for a lunch), V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad; stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- low-sodium deli turkey breast slices: 10 oz (about 15 slices) (300 g)
- Swiss cheese slices: 5 oz (15 slices) (150 g)
- light cream cheese: 3 tbsp (45 g)
- cucumber, cut in thin sticks: 1 medium (150 g)

**Steps**
1. Lay out 15 slices of turkey on a clean board. Spread a thin layer of cream cheese (about 1 tsp) over each slice, leaving a 1/2 in / 1 cm border.
2. Top each with a slice of Swiss and a cucumber stick laid across one end.
3. Roll up tightly from the cucumber end. Done when the roll holds closed on its own without tearing the turkey.
4. Pack 3 roll-ups per container, seam side down, with a sheet of parchment between layers so they don't stick.
5. Keep cold at 40 °F / 4 °C or below and eat within 3 days. Deli meat is a higher-risk food; if you are pregnant or have a weak immune system, heat deli meat to steaming (165 °F / 74 °C) before eating, or choose another snack.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 15 minutes at the end of the session. Make it the same day you shop, since deli meat is best fresh.

**Fix it:**
- Rolls come undone: the cream cheese layer was too thin or the slices too cold and stiff. Let them sit out 5 minutes and spread to the edge.
- Cucumber leaks water: pat the sticks dry on paper towel, or use bell pepper strips instead.

**Swap:** Swap the Swiss for provolone or part-skim mozzarella slices. Dairy-free: skip the cheese and use a smear of hummus. Swap the turkey for roast chicken breast deli slices.
