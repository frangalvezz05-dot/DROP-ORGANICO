### D1 · Spinach-Feta Egg Bites
**Makes:** 5 portions (3 egg bites per portion (about 150 g)) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 40 min · **Oven:** 350 °F / 175 °C
**Per portion:** 220 kcal · 18 g protein · 4 g carbs · 15 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, milk
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 2 bites 30-45 sec, 3 bites 45-60 sec, until hot in the middle (165 °F / 74 °C). From frozen: 90 sec at 50% power, then 30 sec at full.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Plan week 1 (Starter). Pairs with a piece of fruit or a slice of toast.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 10 large eggs (500 g)
- 1/2 cup 2% cottage cheese (113 g)
- 4 packed cups fresh baby spinach (120 g)
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta (75 g)
- 1 tsp olive oil (for the spinach) (4.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (1.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper (0.5 g)
- 1 tsp canola oil or cooking spray (for the tin) (4.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 350 °F / 175 °C. Grease 15 muffin cups well (one 12-cup tin plus 3 cups of a second tin) with oil or spray. Silicone cups work too.
2. Warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the spinach and stir 1-2 min until fully wilted and bright green. Tip into a sieve and press out the water with a spoon. Chop fine.
3. Blend or whisk the eggs and cottage cheese with the garlic powder, salt and pepper until nearly smooth, with only tiny curds.
4. Divide the spinach and feta evenly between the cups, then pour in the egg until each is about 3/4 full.
5. Bake 20-24 min until the tops are puffed and set, the centers don't jiggle when you nudge the tin, and a knife in the middle comes out clean (160 °F / 71 °C).
6. Cool in the tin 5 min, then run a knife around each and lift out. Cool completely before packing 3 per container.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, 22 min at 350 °F. Slot it in beside the main tray (350 °F on the lower rack) or right after the proteins come out.

**Fix it:**
- Rubbery or weeping water? It baked too long or the spinach wasn't squeezed dry. Pull at 20 min and squeeze the spinach harder.
- Stuck to the tin? Grease was too thin. Run a thin knife around each and use silicone cups next time.

**Swap:** Dairy-free: use 1/2 cup extra spinach and 3 Tbsp nutritional yeast in place of the cottage cheese and feta (macros change). Cheaper: swap the feta for 1/2 cup shredded cheddar.
