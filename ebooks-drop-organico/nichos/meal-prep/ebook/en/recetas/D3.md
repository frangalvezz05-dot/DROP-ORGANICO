### D3 · Greek Yogurt Overnight Oats
**Makes:** 5 portions (1 jar (about 330 g)) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 10 min + 6 h chill
**Per portion:** 340 kcal · 24 g protein · 50 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Eat cold, straight from the jar. Or microwave 60-90 sec and stir.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Plan week 2 (Budget). Takes no hands on the morning.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 1/2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats (200 g)
- 2 1/2 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt (600 g)
- 2 1/2 cups 1% milk (610 g)
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp chia seeds (20 g)
- 5 tsp honey (35 g)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (4 g)
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon (2.6 g)
- a pinch fine salt (0.5 g)
- 1 1/4 cups blueberries (fresh or frozen) (185 g)

**Steps**
1. Line up 5 jars or containers of at least 12 oz / 350 ml each.
2. In a large bowl, stir together the oats, chia, cinnamon and salt.
3. Add the yogurt, milk, honey and vanilla. Stir until there are no dry pockets of oats and no yogurt lumps.
4. Divide evenly between the jars (about 1 cup / 285 g each).
5. Top each jar with 1/4 cup berries. Close the lids and refrigerate at least 6 hours.
6. In the morning, stir the jar. It's ready when the oats are soft and the mix looks thick and spoonable like pudding.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 10 minutes, any time on Sunday. Needs 6 hours in the fridge, so make it before dinner.

**Fix it:**
- Too thick? Stir in 2 Tbsp milk before eating.
- Too runny? The oats were quick oats or the jar sat under 6 hours. Add 1 tsp chia and wait 30 min.

**Swap:** Dairy-free: use unsweetened soy milk and a plain soy or coconut yogurt (macros change). Cheaper: use frozen berries or 1 mashed banana instead.
