### S10 · Smoky BBQ Sauce (Less Sugar)
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (40 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 20 min
**Per portion:** 40 kcal · 1 g protein · 10 g carbs · 0 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** fish
**Fridge:** 5 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Warm in a pan or microwave 30 sec. Stir.
**Goes with:** C1, C2, C5, C8, C11 (Comfort plates with S10)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1/3 cup ketchup (85 g)
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste (32 g)
- 2 Tbsp cider vinegar (30 g)
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar (14 g)
- 1 tsp yellow mustard (5 g)
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (5 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.3 g)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (1.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp onion powder (1.2 g)
- a pinch cayenne (0.2 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)
- 1/4 cup water (60 g)

**Steps**
1. Whisk everything in a small saucepan: ketchup, tomato paste, vinegar, brown sugar, mustard, Worcestershire, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, salt and water.
2. Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat, then turn to low. Simmer 10-12 min, stirring every few minutes.
3. It's done when a spoon leaves a clear trail across the bottom of the pan and the sauce looks dark and glossy.
4. Taste. More cayenne for heat, more vinegar for tang. Cool 10 min.
5. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 40 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire. One burner, 20 min, mostly unattended on low. Start it first.

**Fix it:**
- Splattering? Turn the heat down and half-cover the pan.
- Too tangy? Stir in 1 tsp honey. Too thick when cold? Whisk in 1 tsp water.

**Swap:** No fish allergen: use 1 tsp soy sauce in place of Worcestershire (adds soy and wheat). Lower sugar still: use 2 tsp brown sugar.
