### V5 · Sesame Cabbage-Carrot Slaw (raw)

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 130 g cooked each) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 15 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 110 kcal · 2 g protein · 13 g carbs · 7 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** sesame, soy, wheat
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Not reheated. Eat cold. Keep the dressing in a jar and toss on the day for crunch.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M5, M8, A2, A3, A8 · flavour families ASI, MEX, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1/2 small head (about 1 lb), thinly shredded green cabbage (450 g)
- 2 large, grated carrots (150 g)
- 3, thinly sliced scallions (40 g)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (14 g)
- 1 tbsp canola oil (14 g)
- 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar (45 g)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (16 g)
- 1 tbsp honey (21 g)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (9 g)
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Shred the cabbage as thin as you can with a sharp knife. Grate the carrots. Slice the scallions.
2. In a jar or bowl, whisk the sesame oil, canola oil, vinegar, soy sauce, honey, ginger and half the sesame seeds until the honey dissolves.
3. Combine the vegetables in a big bowl. Keep the dressing apart for now.
4. Portion the vegetables into 5 containers (about 130 g each) with a small cup or section for the dressing (about 1 1/2 tbsp each).
5. When eating, pour the dressing over and toss. Top with the rest of the sesame seeds.
6. Taste the dressing: it should be tangy and a bit sweet. Add a pinch of salt or 1 tsp more honey to taste.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold prep, no heat. Do it while the oven runs. Dress it only on days 1-2 if you want maximum crunch.
**Fix it:**
- Watery slaw: it was dressed too early. Keep the dressing separate and drain any liquid.
- Too sharp: add 1 tsp honey or 1 tbsp oil.

**Swap:** Allergy to sesame: leave out the seeds and use 1 tbsp more canola oil. Gluten-free: use tamari. Use pre-shredded coleslaw mix to save 8 minutes.
