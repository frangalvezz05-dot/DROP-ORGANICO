### S2 · Tomatillo Salsa Verde
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (45 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 20 min · **Oven:** broil, high
**Per portion:** 20 kcal · 0 g protein · 3 g carbs · 0 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $0.25 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Eat cold or at room temperature. For a warm sauce, simmer 3 min in a small pan.
**Goes with:** M2, M5, M7, M9 (Mexican plates with S2)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 3-4 medium tomatillos, husked and rinsed (170 g)
- 1 jalapeño, stem removed (15 g)
- about 1/4 small white onion, quartered (30 g)
- 1 clove garlic, in the skin (3 g)
- 1/4 cup packed fresh cilantro, leaves and tender stems (8 g)
- 1 tsp lime juice (5 g)
- 1/3 tsp fine salt (2 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the broiler on high with a rack 4-6 inches below the heat. Line a small sheet pan with foil.
2. Put the tomatillos, jalapeño, onion and unpeeled garlic on the pan. Broil 5-7 min until the tops are blistered and spotted black.
3. Flip everything. Broil 4-5 min more until the tomatillos are soft, slumped and leaking juice. The garlic should feel soft when squeezed.
4. Cool 5 min. Slip the garlic out of its skin. Tip everything, juices too, into a blender with cilantro, lime juice and salt.
5. Pulse 8-10 times for a bit of texture, or blend longer for smooth. Taste and add salt until it tastes bright, not flat.
6. Pour into a lidded jar or 5 mini containers (about 3 Tbsp / 45 g each). Cool fully before closing.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven (broiler) for 12 min. Fits in the last 20 minutes while the main tray rests, or right after the main tray comes out.

**Fix it:**
- Too sour or sharp? Tomatillos vary. Stir in 1/4 tsp honey or sugar.
- Too watery? Simmer in a pan 4-5 min, stirring, until it coats the back of a spoon.

**Swap:** Mild: take the seeds and ribs out of the jalapeño. No broiler: pan-roast in a dry cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat, turning, 10 min. Not hot enough? Add a second jalapeño.
