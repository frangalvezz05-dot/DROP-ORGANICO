### B2 · Fluffy Quinoa

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 168 g cooked each) · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 25 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 210 kcal · 7 g protein · 33 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 60-90 sec with a splash of water, covered, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).
**Goes with:** signature dishes M6, A2, A4, A12, D-2, D-3, D-5, D-7 · flavour families ASI, MED, MEX, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1 1/2 cups quinoa (255 g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (4.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Rinse the quinoa in a fine sieve for 30 seconds, rubbing it with your hand. This removes the bitter coating.
2. Add the quinoa, 3 cups water, oil and salt to a medium pot. Bring to a boil.
3. Lower to the lowest heat, cover and cook 14-16 minutes. Done when the water is gone and you can see the little white spiral tails on each seed.
4. Take off the heat, keep the lid on and leave 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
5. Spread on a tray to cool 15 minutes. Portion into 5 containers (about 165 g each) and refrigerate within 1 hour.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, minute 0 to 20, one pot with a lid. Hands free after the first 5 minutes.
**Fix it:**
- Mushy: too much water or you lifted the lid. Next time stick to 2 cups of water per 1 cup of quinoa.
- Bitter: it was not rinsed enough.

**Swap:** Use millet or couscous in the same weight if quinoa is too pricey. Cheaper: half quinoa, half rice, cooked separately.
