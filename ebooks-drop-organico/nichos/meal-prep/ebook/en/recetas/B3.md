### B3 · Crispy Roasted Baby Potatoes

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 170 g cooked each) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 45 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 190 kcal · 4 g protein · 33 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Oven or air fryer, 400 °F / 205 °C for 8-10 minutes. Microwave makes them soft.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M9, M12, D-11, C1, C5, C6 · flavour families MEX, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2.2 lb baby red potatoes, halved (1000 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Put the empty sheet pan in the oven to heat.
2. Halve the potatoes (quarter any bigger than a golf ball). Dry them with a towel.
3. Toss in a bowl with the oil, salt, garlic powder, paprika and pepper.
4. Carefully tip onto the hot pan, cut sides down. They should sizzle.
5. Roast 20 minutes, flip, then roast 15-20 minutes more. Done when the cut sides are deep golden and a knife slides in with no push.
6. Cool, then portion into 5 containers (about 170 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 40 (long tray, so put it in first). Goes on the lower rack at 425 °F / 220 °C.
**Fix it:**
- Soggy: they were wet or the pan was crowded. Dry them and use two pans.
- Hard centres, burnt edges: pieces were uneven. Cut to the same size, and give the big ones 10 minutes extra.

**Swap:** Use Yukon Gold potatoes cut into 1-inch chunks. Not good for the freezer: they turn grainy and watery.
