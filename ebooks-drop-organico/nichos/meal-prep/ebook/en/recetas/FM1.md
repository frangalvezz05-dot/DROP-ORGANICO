### FM1 · Turkey Three-Bean Chili
**Makes:** 6 portions (~540 g each) · **Active:** 25 min · **Total:** 60 min
**Per portion:** 480 kcal · 42 g protein · 44 g carbs · 18 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $3.20 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Reheat covered in the microwave 3-4 min, stirring halfway, or in a pot over medium 6-8 min, until steaming and 165 °F / 74 °C in the center.
**Goes with:** B1 Hands-Off Oven Rice, S1 Chipotle-Lime Yogurt, V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- lean ground turkey (93/7): 2 lb (907 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- yellow onion, diced: 1 large (200 g)
- red bell pepper, diced: 1 large (150 g)
- garlic, minced: 4 cloves (12 g)
- chili powder: 2 tbsp (15 g)
- ground cumin: 2 tsp (5 g)
- smoked paprika: 1 tsp (3 g)
- crushed tomatoes: 1 can (28 oz) (794 g)
- tomato paste: 2 tbsp (32 g)
- kidney beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- black beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- pinto beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- frozen corn kernels: 1 cup (150 g)
- low-sodium chicken broth: 2 cups (480 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oil in a large pot (5-6 qt / 5-6 L) over medium-high heat. Add the onion and pepper and cook 5 minutes, stirring, until the onion is soft and see-through at the edges.
2. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds, just until you can smell it. Push the vegetables to the sides, add the turkey, and break it up with a spoon. Cook 6-8 minutes until no pink is left and it looks like small crumbles.
3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, paprika and tomato paste. Cook 1 minute until the paste darkens a shade and the spices smell toasty.
4. Add the crushed tomatoes, all three beans, corn, broth and salt. Bring to a boil, then drop to low and simmer, partly covered, 30 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes. It is done when a spoon dragged across the bottom leaves a clear trail for a second and the chili coats the spoon.
5. Taste and add salt in pinches if needed. Check that the chili is at least 165 °F / 74 °C in the middle.
6. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Chili freezes well in zip bags laid flat: they stack and thaw in half the time.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop. Start at minute 0 on a back burner; it simmers on its own from minute 20 to minute 50, so the oven and the other burners stay free for P and B recipes.

**Fix it:**
- Too thin: simmer uncovered 10 more minutes, or mash a half cup of the beans against the pot wall and stir them in.
- Tastes flat: add 1 tsp vinegar or lime juice off the heat and a pinch more salt. Acid wakes up tomato-based dishes.

**Swap:** Swap the turkey for 2 lb of 90% lean ground beef (brown it until no pink remains and the internal temp reads 160 °F / 71 °C). For a budget version, replace one can of beans with 1 cup of cooked lentils.
