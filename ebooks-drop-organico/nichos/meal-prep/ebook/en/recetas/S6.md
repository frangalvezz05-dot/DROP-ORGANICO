### S6 · Gochujang-Honey Sauce
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (30 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 10 min
**Per portion:** 30 kcal · 1 g protein · 6 g carbs · 1 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** soy, wheat, sesame
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Serve cool or warm 20 sec in the microwave. It thickens in the fridge; stir in a splash of water.
**Goes with:** A3, A6, A7, A9, A11 (Asian plates with S6)

*Macro note:* USDA has no gochujang entry. It is costed with the closest entry (sriracha chili sauce), which likely UNDERSTATES calories by roughly 10-15 kcal per portion. Check your jar's label.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 3 Tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste) (50 g)
- 1 Tbsp honey (21 g)
- 1 Tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce (16 g)
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar or cider vinegar (15 g)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (4.5 g)
- 2 cloves garlic, grated (6 g)
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely grated (2 g)
- 3 Tbsp water (45 g)

**Steps**
1. In a small saucepan, whisk the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, ginger and water until smooth.
2. Heat over medium-low, stirring, until small bubbles break at the edges, about 2-3 min. Do not boil hard; it spits.
3. Simmer 1 min until it looks glossy and slightly thicker. Take it off the heat and stir in the sesame oil.
4. Cool 10 min. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2 Tbsp / 30 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire. One small burner, 8 min. Fits any gap.

**Fix it:**
- Too spicy? Stir in 1 tsp more honey and 1 tsp water.
- Too salty? Gochujang brands differ. Add 1 tsp water and 1 tsp vinegar next time, and use less soy sauce.

**Swap:** Gluten-free: check the gochujang label (many brands contain wheat) and use tamari. Milder: swap 1 Tbsp of gochujang for tomato paste.
