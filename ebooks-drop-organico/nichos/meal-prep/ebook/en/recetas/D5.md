### D5 · Freezer Breakfast Burritos
**Makes:** 5 portions (1 burrito (about 255 g)) · **Active:** 25 min · **Total:** 35 min
**Per portion:** 520 kcal · 28 g protein · 43 g carbs · 26 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, milk, wheat
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** From frozen: unwrap, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave 2 min, flip, then 45-60 sec more until hot in the middle (165 °F / 74 °C). Or thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave 60-90 sec.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Plan week 3 (Freezer-ahead). Serve with salsa S2 or S1 on the side (not counted).

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 10 large eggs (500 g)
- 5 10-inch flour tortillas (325 g)
- 1 cup canned low-sodium black beans, drained and rinsed (215 g)
- 1 1/4 cups shredded sharp cheddar (140 g)
- 1 medium red bell pepper, finely diced (120 g)
- 1/3 cup yellow onion, finely diced (50 g)
- 2 tsp olive oil (9 g)
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin (1 g)
- 3/4 tsp fine salt (4.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper (0.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Warm the oil in a large nonstick pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper 3-4 min until the edges soften. Stir in the cumin, salt and pepper.
2. Beat the eggs in a bowl. Pour them into the pan and stir gently, folding, until the eggs form large soft curds and no wet shine is left, 3-4 min. Take it off the heat; it keeps cooking.
3. Cool the egg mix 10 min on a plate. Hot filling steams the tortillas soggy.
4. Warm the tortillas 10 sec each in the microwave so they fold without cracking. Lay one flat and put 1/5 of the eggs, 3 Tbsp beans and 1/5 of the cheddar in a line below the center.
5. Fold the sides in, then roll up tight from the bottom. Repeat for all 5.
6. Wrap each burrito in parchment or foil. For the freezer, put them in a bag, press out the air and flatten. Label with the date.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire. One pan, 15 min of active time. Do it while the oven is full; no oven needed.

**Fix it:**
- Soggy burrito? The filling was warm when you rolled. Cool it fully first and drain the beans well.
- Eggs dry after reheating? Cook them softer (still set, a little glossy) and reheat covered with a damp towel.

**Swap:** Dairy-free: omit the cheddar and add 1/2 cup more beans. Gluten-free: use large corn tortillas or serve as a bowl (macros change). More protein: add 4 oz / 115 g cooked turkey crumble (P4) to the mix.
