### SN4 · Peanut Butter Oat Bites
**Makes:** 5 portions (3 bites (about 70 g)) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 45 min
**Per portion:** 330 kcal · 11 g protein · 38 g carbs · 17 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** peanuts
**Cost/portion:** about $0.60 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 7 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Eat cold or at room temperature. To freeze, set the bites on a tray for 1 hour, then bag them. Thaw 15 minutes on the counter or overnight in the fridge. (No raw egg or meat, so counter thawing is safe here.)
**Goes with:** D4 Peanut Butter-Banana Overnight Oats; stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- old-fashioned rolled oats: 1 1/4 cups (120 g)
- smooth peanut butter: 1/2 cup (128 g)
- honey: 1/4 cup (85 g)
- ground flaxseed: 2 tbsp (20 g)
- unsweetened cocoa powder: 1 tbsp (8 g)
- vanilla extract: 1 tsp (4 g)
- kosher salt: 1/4 tsp (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Line a tray or plate with parchment. Mix the oats, flax, cocoa and salt in a bowl.
2. Warm the peanut butter and honey together in the microwave for 20 seconds (or in a small pot on low for 1 minute). Stir in the vanilla.
3. Pour the wet mix over the oats and stir until no dry oats remain. It should hold together when you squeeze a spoonful. If it is crumbly, add 1 tsp water; if it is sticky, add 1 tbsp oats.
4. Chill the bowl 15 minutes so the mix firms up and is easy to roll.
5. Roll into 15 balls, about 1 tbsp (25 g) each, and put them on the tray. Done when each ball is firm and holds its shape without leaving a greasy mark on your palm.
6. Chill 30 minutes more, then store in an airtight container. Divide into 5 portions of 3.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold, no cooking. 15 minutes of work. Do it while an oven item bakes and finish the chilling in the fridge.

**Fix it:**
- Too crumbly: add 1 tsp water or 1 tsp honey, mix, and test again.
- Too sticky to roll: chill the bowl 15 more minutes, or wet your hands lightly.

**Swap:** Peanut-free: use sunflower seed butter (same amount). Lower sugar: use 3 tbsp honey and add 1 tbsp more oats.
