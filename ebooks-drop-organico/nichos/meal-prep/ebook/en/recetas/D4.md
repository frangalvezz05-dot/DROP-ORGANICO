### D4 · Peanut Butter-Banana Overnight Oats
**Makes:** 5 portions (1 jar, about 290 g) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 10 min + 6 h chill
**Per portion:** 400 kcal · 22 g protein · 51 g carbs · 14 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk, peanuts
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Eat cold. Or microwave 60-90 sec, stir, and add a splash of milk.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Banana is mashed in, so it doesn't brown. Add a few banana slices on top the morning you eat it if you like.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 1/2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats (200 g)
- 2 1/2 cups 1% milk (610 g)
- 1 1/4 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt (300 g)
- 5 Tbsp smooth peanut butter (no sugar added) (80 g)
- 2 medium ripe bananas, mashed (236 g)
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp chia seeds (20 g)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (4 g)
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon (2.6 g)
- a pinch fine salt (0.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Line up 5 jars of at least 12 oz / 350 ml each.
2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until almost smooth. Stir in the peanut butter until it's mostly blended.
3. Whisk in the milk, then the yogurt, vanilla, cinnamon and salt until the mix is an even light tan.
4. Stir in the oats and chia.
5. Divide evenly between the jars (about 1 1/4 cups / 290 g each). Close and refrigerate at least 6 hours.
6. In the morning, stir. It's ready when the oats are soft and no dry oats show at the bottom.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 10 minutes, any time. Needs 6 hours in the fridge, so make it before dinner.

**Fix it:**
- Peanut butter lumps? Warm the spoon in hot water or microwave the peanut butter 10 sec before mixing.
- Too thick? Stir in 2 Tbsp milk. Too sweet? Use one banana less.

**Swap:** Peanut allergy: use sunflower seed butter (macros change). Dairy-free: soy milk and plain soy yogurt (macros change).
