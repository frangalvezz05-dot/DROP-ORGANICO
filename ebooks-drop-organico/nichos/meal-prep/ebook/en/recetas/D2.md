### D2 · Turkey & Pepper Egg Bites
**Makes:** 5 portions (3 egg bites per portion, about 170 g) · **Active:** 20 min · **Total:** 45 min · **Oven:** 350 °F / 175 °C
**Per portion:** 280 kcal · 24 g protein · 3 g carbs · 19 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, milk
**Cost/portion:** about $1.20 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 2 bites 30-45 sec, 3 bites 45-60 sec, until hot in the middle (165 °F / 74 °C). From frozen: 90 sec at 50% power, then 30 sec at full.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Plan week 4 (Mix-it-yourself). Good with salsa S2 on the side.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 10 large eggs (500 g)
- 8 oz ground turkey (93% lean) (227 g)
- 1 medium red bell pepper, finely diced (120 g)
- 1/4 cup yellow onion, finely diced (40 g)
- 1/2 cup shredded sharp cheddar (55 g)
- 1 tsp olive oil (4.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika (1.2 g)
- 3/4 tsp fine salt (4.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper (0.5 g)
- 1 tsp canola oil or cooking spray (for the tin) (4.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 350 °F / 175 °C. Grease 15 muffin cups well.
2. Warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and pepper. Cook 3 min until the pepper just softens.
3. Add the turkey, paprika and half the salt. Break it into small crumbs with a spoon. Cook 5-6 min until no pink remains and a thermometer reads 165 °F / 74 °C. Drain off any liquid. Cool 5 min.
4. Whisk the eggs with the rest of the salt and the pepper until fully mixed.
5. Divide the turkey mix and cheddar between the cups. Pour in egg until each is about 3/4 full.
6. Bake 20-24 min until puffed, set in the middle, and a knife comes out clean (160 °F / 71 °C). Cool 5 min in the tin, lift out, and cool fully before packing 3 per container.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire first (10 min for the turkey), then oven 22 min at 350 °F beside the main tray.

**Fix it:**
- Watery bottoms? The turkey liquid wasn't drained. Drain it and blot with a paper towel.
- Dry? It baked past 24 min. Pull when the centers are just set.

**Swap:** Dairy-free: leave out the cheddar and add 1/2 tsp extra salt and 1 Tbsp nutritional yeast. Cheaper: use ground chicken or leftover cooked crumble from P4.
