### FM7 · Teriyaki Chicken Fried Rice
**Makes:** 6 portions (~310 g each) · **Active:** 30 min · **Total:** 45 min
**Per portion:** 500 kcal · 38 g protein · 54 g carbs · 14 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, soy, wheat, sesame
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Microwave covered with 1 tbsp water 2-3 min, stirring halfway, until steaming and 165 °F / 74 °C in the center; or reheat in a skillet over medium-high 4-5 min.
**Goes with:** V1 Charred Roasted Broccoli, V5 Sesame Cabbage-Carrot Slaw, S6 Gochujang-Honey

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- long-grain white rice, dry (cook ahead and chill): 1.5 cups (300 g)
- boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut in 3/4 in cubes: 1.5 lb (700 g)
- canola oil: 2 tbsp (28 g)
- toasted sesame oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- large eggs, beaten: 4 (200 g)
- frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn, green beans): 1 bag (14 oz) (400 g)
- scallions, sliced: 4 (50 g)
- garlic, minced: 3 cloves (9 g)
- fresh ginger, grated: 1 tbsp (10 g)
- teriyaki sauce: 6 tbsp (100 g)
- low-sodium soy sauce: 2 tbsp (36 g)

**Steps**
1. Cook the rice ahead: 1.5 cups rice with 3 cups water, covered, 15-18 minutes until all the water is absorbed. Spread it on a sheet pan to cool 20 minutes, then chill it uncovered in the fridge at least 1 hour (cold, dry rice fries better and stays safe). Use it within 24 hours.
2. Heat 1 tbsp canola oil in your largest skillet or wok over high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook 5-6 minutes, turning, until golden and white inside (165 °F / 74 °C). Move to a bowl.
3. Add the other 1 tbsp canola oil, pour in the eggs, and scramble 1-2 minutes until fully set with no runny parts. Move them to the chicken bowl.
4. Add the sesame oil, garlic, ginger and the white parts of the scallions. Cook 30 seconds. Add the frozen vegetables and cook 3 minutes until hot and bright.
5. Add the cold rice, breaking up clumps. Stir-fry 4 minutes until the rice is hot and a few grains look toasted. Pour in the teriyaki and soy sauce and toss 1 minute.
6. Return the chicken and eggs. Toss 1 minute until everything is steaming. Stir in the green scallion tops. If your pan is small, cook in 2 batches. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Fried rice freezes best in flat, thin layers; press it to about 1 in / 2.5 cm thick.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, last. Cook the rice the night before, so on Sunday this is a 20 minute active job once the other burners are free.

**Fix it:**
- Mushy, clumpy rice: the rice was warm or too wet. Chill it fully, uncovered, and use a hot pan.
- Bland: add 1 tsp soy sauce at the end, not more teriyaki (it is sweet). A squeeze of lime lifts it.

**Swap:** Swap the rice for quinoa (B2) or cauliflower rice (lower carbs, cut the rice and add 4 cups). Gluten-free: use tamari and a gluten-free teriyaki.
