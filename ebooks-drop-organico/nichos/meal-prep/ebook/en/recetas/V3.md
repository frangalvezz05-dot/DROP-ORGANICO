### V3 · Roasted Zucchini & Red Onion

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 130 g cooked each) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 30 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 90 kcal · 2 g protein · 9 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $0.75 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Eat cold, or microwave 45 sec. Best on the first 3 days; it gets watery after.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M4, M9, D-2, D-3, D-9, C3, C9, C10 · flavour families MEX, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 3 medium (about 1.7 lb), cut in 1/2-inch half-moons zucchini (750 g)
- 1 large red onion, cut in wedges (200 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 1 tsp dried oregano (1.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder (1.5 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (15 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C.
2. Cut the zucchini in 1/2-inch half-moons. Cut the onion into thick wedges, keeping the root so the layers hold together.
3. Toss with the oil, oregano, garlic powder and salt.
4. Spread over a large pan in one layer. Zucchini likes space and high heat.
5. Roast 20-25 minutes, turning once at 12 minutes. Done when the edges are golden brown and the middle is tender, not soft and mushy.
6. Add the lemon juice. Portion into 5 containers (about 130 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 25 at 425 °F / 220 °C. Zucchini goes in last in the week-1 order, as it turns watery the longest in the fridge.
**Fix it:**
- Mushy and wet: pan crowded or pieces too thin. Next time keep them thick and use 2 pans.
- Watery by day 3: normal. Pat dry with a paper towel before eating.

**Swap:** Use yellow squash, or eggplant cubes (add 5 minutes). Add 1/2 tsp of smoked paprika for a stronger taste.
