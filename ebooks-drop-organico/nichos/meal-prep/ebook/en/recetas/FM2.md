### FM2 · Chicken Tikka-Style Curry
**Makes:** 6 portions (~525 g each) · **Active:** 25 min · **Total:** 55 min
**Per portion:** 550 kcal · 46 g protein · 49 g carbs · 20 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk (coconut milk too)
**Cost/portion:** about $2.85 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Microwave covered 3-4 min, stirring halfway, adding 1 tbsp water to the rice; or reheat the curry in a pan over medium 6-8 min. It must reach 165 °F / 74 °C in the center.
**Goes with:** B1 Hands-Off Oven Rice (the rice is already packed in), V5 Sesame Cabbage-Carrot Slaw, S7 Tzatziki

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut in 1.5 in chunks: 2.2 lb (1000 g)
- plain nonfat Greek yogurt: 1 cup (245 g)
- curry powder: 1 tbsp (7 g)
- kosher salt: 1.5 tsp (8 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- yellow onion, diced: 1 large (250 g)
- garlic, minced: 4 cloves (12 g)
- fresh ginger, grated: 1 tbsp (10 g)
- smoked paprika: 2 tsp (5 g)
- ground turmeric: 1 tsp (3 g)
- tomato paste: 3 tbsp (48 g)
- crushed tomatoes: 1 can (14.5 oz) (411 g)
- canned coconut milk: 1 cup (half a can) (240 g)
- chickpeas, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- fresh baby spinach: 5 oz (140 g)
- long-grain white rice, cooked (to pack with each portion): about 1/2 cup / 85 g per portion (510 g)

_Nutrition note: includes 85 g of cooked rice per portion_

**Steps**
1. Mix the chicken with the yogurt, curry powder and 1 tsp of the salt in a bowl. Let it sit 15 minutes on the counter while you chop (no longer than 30 minutes; for longer, marinate in the fridge).
2. Heat the oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes until soft with golden edges. Add garlic and ginger and cook 30 seconds.
3. Stir in paprika, turmeric and tomato paste. Cook 1-2 minutes until the paste turns a deeper brick red.
4. Add the chicken with all its marinade, the crushed tomatoes and the coconut milk. Stir, bring to a gentle bubble, then lower to medium-low and simmer, partly covered, 15 minutes. The chicken is done when the biggest piece is white all the way through and reads 165 °F / 74 °C.
5. Stir in the chickpeas and spinach and the rest of the salt. Cook 2-3 minutes until the spinach has wilted into the sauce and the sauce coats the back of a spoon. Do not boil hard once the yogurt is in, or it can split.
6. Cook the rice (or use the rice from B1). Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: pack each of 6 containers with a scoop of rice (about 1/2 cup / 85 g) and a scoop of curry, side by side. Date the lids and freeze flat.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop. Starts at minute 10 (after the oven things are in) and needs about 40 minutes with the lid half on, so it can share the back burner with a rice pot.

**Fix it:**
- Sauce split or grainy: you boiled it after the yogurt went in. Take it off the heat, whisk in 2 tbsp warm water, and keep it at a bare simmer from now on.
- Chicken tough: the chunks were too small or cooked too hot. Cut them at least 1.5 in / 4 cm and keep the simmer gentle.

**Swap:** Swap thighs for boneless skinless chicken breast (same weight); cook it just until it hits 165 °F / 74 °C. Dairy-free: use coconut yogurt for the marinade and skip nothing else. Nut-free note: coconut is not a tree nut in most guidance, but check with your doctor if you have a tree-nut allergy.
