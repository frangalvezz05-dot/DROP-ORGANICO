### P7 · Oven Pulled Pork Shoulder

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 156 g cooked each) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 300 min · **Oven:** 300 °F / 150 °C
**Per portion:** 430 kcal · 54 g protein · 5 g carbs · 20 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 90 sec with 1 tbsp of its juices until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).
**Goes with:** signature dishes M5, A3, A12, C2, C11 · flavour families MEX, COM, ASI

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 3 lb, after trimming (buy about 3 3/4 lb) trimmed boneless pork shoulder (butt) (1361 g)
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (13 g)
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika (7 g)
- 2 tsp ground cumin (4 g)
- 2 tsp garlic powder (6 g)
- 2 1/2 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (15 g)
- 1 tsp black pepper (2 g)
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar (58 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 300 °F / 150 °C. Trim off the thick fat cap and any big fat seams you can reach with a knife (the macros here are for trimmed meat). Mix the sugar, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Rub it all over the pork.
2. Place the pork in a Dutch oven or a deep baking dish. Pour in the vinegar and 1/2 cup water around it, not over it.
3. Cover tightly with a lid or double foil. Roast 4 to 4 1/2 hours.
4. Done when a thermometer reads 195-205 °F / 90-96 °C and a fork twists in with no resistance. Safe at 145 °F, but it only shreds once the connective tissue melts at the higher temperature.
5. Rest 20 minutes, covered. Move to a board. Discard any bone and leftover fat. Shred with two forks.
6. Skim the fat from the pan juices. Toss the pork with 1/2 cup of the juices. Portion into 5 containers (about 155 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 270. Start it first. At 300 °F it blocks the oven for 4-4.5 hours, so P7 is a slow-Sunday protein: pair it with stovetop bases and cold veg, or cook it Saturday and shred on Sunday.
**Fix it:**
- Pork will not shred: it needs more time. Re-cover and give it another 30-45 minutes.
- Dry after reheating: always store with its juices; add 1 tbsp water to the container before the microwave.

**Swap:** Same recipe in a slow cooker: LOW 8-10 hours, no extra liquid except the vinegar. Leaner and faster: use pork tenderloin (cook to 145 °F), but it will not be as juicy.
