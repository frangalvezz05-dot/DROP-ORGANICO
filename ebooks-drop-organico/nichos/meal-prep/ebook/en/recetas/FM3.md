### FM3 · Beef & Lentil Ragù
**Makes:** 6 portions (~490 g each) · **Active:** 25 min · **Total:** 65 min
**Per portion:** 560 kcal · 37 g protein · 72 g carbs · 15 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat, milk
**Cost/portion:** about $3.05 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Reheat in a pot over medium 6-8 min (or microwave covered 3-4 min), stirring, until 165 °F / 74 °C in the center. Meanwhile boil 1.75 oz / 50 g dry pasta per portion for the time on the package minus 1 minute, and toss it with the hot ragù. Nutrition below includes this pasta and the Parmesan.
**Goes with:** B5 Reheat-Ready Pasta (use instead of fresh pasta if you prefer), V1 Charred Roasted Broccoli, V3 Roasted Zucchini & Red Onion

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- lean ground beef (90/10): 1.25 lb (567 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- yellow onion, finely diced: 1 large (200 g)
- carrots, finely diced: 2 medium (130 g)
- celery, finely diced: 2 stalks (80 g)
- garlic, minced: 4 cloves (12 g)
- tomato paste: 3 tbsp (48 g)
- dried oregano: 2 tsp (3 g)
- brown or green lentils, dry, rinsed: 3/4 cup (150 g)
- crushed tomatoes: 1 can (28 oz) (794 g)
- low-sodium beef broth: 2 cups (480 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)
- black pepper: 1/2 tsp (1 g)
- dry spaghetti or penne (cooked fresh on serving day, about 1.75 oz / 50 g dry per portion): about 10.5 oz total (300 g)
- grated Parmesan (1 tbsp per portion): 6 tbsp (30 g)

_Nutrition note: includes the pasta (50 g dry per portion) and 1 tbsp Parmesan, added on serving day_

**Steps**
1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the beef and break it into small crumbles. Cook 7-8 minutes until all the pink is gone and the bits are lightly browned. Spoon off most of the fat.
2. Add onion, carrot and celery. Cook 6 minutes, stirring, until the onion is soft and see-through. Add garlic and oregano, 30 seconds.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 1 minute until it darkens a shade.
4. Add the lentils, crushed tomatoes, broth, salt and pepper. Bring to a boil, lower to a steady simmer, cover with the lid slightly open, and cook 35-40 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes. It is done when a lentil squashes easily between two fingers and the sauce is thick enough to hold a spoon trail.
5. If it looks dry before the lentils are soft, add 1/2 cup water and keep going. Check that it reads 160 °F / 71 °C or more in the center.
6. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Freeze the ragù only. Do not freeze it mixed with pasta; pasta turns mushy.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop. Start at minute 5; it simmers hands-off from minute 25 to minute 65, which suits the long, quiet part of a Sunday.

**Fix it:**
- Lentils still hard after 40 minutes: your lentils are old or your tomatoes are very acidic. Add 1/2 cup water, cover, and cook 10 more minutes before tasting again.
- Greasy surface: skim it off with a spoon before portioning, or chill the ragù and lift off the white fat that sets on top.

**Swap:** Swap the beef for lean ground turkey (cook to 165 °F / 74 °C). Gluten-free: serve over gluten-free pasta or baked sweet potato. Budget: use 1 lb of beef and 1 cup (200 g) lentils.
