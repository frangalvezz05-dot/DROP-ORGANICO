### FM8 · Turkey Meatball & Orzo Bake
**Makes:** 6 portions (~380 g each) · **Active:** 30 min · **Total:** 65 min · **Oven:** 400 °F / 205 °C
**Per portion:** 530 kcal · 39 g protein · 53 g carbs · 19 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, wheat, milk
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Microwave covered 3-4 min with 1 tbsp water on top, or bake covered at 350 °F / 175 °C for 15-20 min, until 165 °F / 74 °C in the center.
**Goes with:** V3 Roasted Zucchini & Red Onion, V4 Garlic Green Beans, S9 Chimichurri

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- lean ground turkey (93/7): 1.5 lb (680 g)
- large egg: 1 (50 g)
- plain dry breadcrumbs: 1/3 cup (40 g)
- grated Parmesan: 1/4 cup (25 g)
- yellow onion, grated: 1/2 medium (75 g)
- garlic, minced: 4 cloves (12 g)
- dried oregano: 1 tsp (2 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)
- marinara sauce, no sugar added: 1 jar (24 oz) (680 g)
- low-sodium chicken broth: 2 cups (480 g)
- dry orzo (or other small pasta): 1.5 cups (270 g)
- baby spinach, chopped: 4 oz (115 g)
- shredded part-skim mozzarella: 1 cup (4 oz) (115 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 400 °F / 200 °C. Mix the turkey, egg, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, onion, garlic, oregano and salt with your hands until just combined (overmixing makes tough meatballs). Roll into 24 meatballs, about 1.5 in / 4 cm each.
2. Stir the marinara, broth and orzo together in a 9x13 in / 23x33 cm baking dish. Stir in the spinach. Nestle the meatballs on top.
3. Cover tightly with foil. Bake 30 minutes.
4. Uncover, stir the orzo gently (it can stick at the edges), and scatter the mozzarella on top. Bake 10-12 more minutes until the cheese is melted with browned spots, the orzo is tender with no hard center, and a meatball cut open reads 165 °F / 74 °C and shows no pink.
5. Rest 10 minutes; the orzo soaks up the last of the sauce. Cut into 6 portions (4 meatballs each).
6. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Freeze in single-portion containers; the orzo is softer after thawing, which is normal.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven for 40-45 min at 400 °F / 200 °C. Slot it with the 425 °F sheet-pan proteins; the stovetop stays free for soups.

**Fix it:**
- Orzo crunchy in the middle: the dish was not covered tight or the oven runs cool. Add 1/4 cup hot water, cover again, and bake 8 more minutes.
- Dry meatballs: you used too much breadcrumb or mixed too long. Keep the 1.5 lb turkey to 1/3 cup crumbs and mix just until combined.

**Swap:** Swap the orzo for the same weight of any small pasta, or for brown rice (needs 50 minutes covered). Dairy-free: skip the Parmesan and mozzarella and add 1/2 tsp salt.
