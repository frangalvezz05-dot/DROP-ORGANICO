### FM4 · Chicken Enchilada Bake
**Makes:** 6 portions (~390 g each) · **Active:** 30 min · **Total:** 70 min · **Oven:** 375 °F / 190 °C
**Per portion:** 550 kcal · 50 g protein · 45 g carbs · 19 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Cost/portion:** about $3.20 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Microwave 2-3 min on a plate, covered, until steaming; or bake at 350 °F / 175 °C for 15-20 min covered with foil. Center must reach 165 °F / 74 °C.
**Goes with:** V2 Sheet-Pan Fajita Peppers & Onions, B1 Hands-Off Oven Rice, S1 Chipotle-Lime Yogurt

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- boneless skinless chicken breasts: 2 lb (907 g)
- red enchilada sauce, mild: 3 cups (24 oz) (680 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- yellow onion, diced: 1 medium (150 g)
- ground cumin: 2 tsp (5 g)
- chili powder: 1 tbsp (8 g)
- black beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- frozen corn kernels: 1 cup (150 g)
- corn tortillas (6 in): 12 (312 g)
- shredded Mexican-blend cheese: 1.5 cups (6 oz) (170 g)
- plain nonfat Greek yogurt (stirred into the filling): 1/2 cup (125 g)
- kosher salt: 1/2 tsp (3 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 375 °F / 190 °C. Lightly oil a 9x13 in / 23x33 cm baking dish.
2. In a large skillet, heat the oil over medium and cook the onion 4 minutes until soft. Add cumin and chili powder for 30 seconds. Pour in 2 cups of the enchilada sauce, add the whole chicken breasts, cover, and simmer on low 18-25 minutes, flipping once, until the thickest part reads 165 °F / 74 °C and the juices run clear.
3. Move the chicken to a board and shred it with two forks. Return it to the skillet with the beans, corn and salt, and stir. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the yogurt (off the heat so it does not curdle).
4. Spread 1/2 cup sauce on the bottom of the dish. Layer 4 tortillas (overlap them), then 1/3 of the filling, then 1/4 of the cheese. Repeat twice. Finish with the last 4 tortillas, the last of the sauce, and the rest of the cheese.
5. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Uncover and bake 10-15 minutes more until the cheese is melted with a few browned spots and the sauce bubbles at the edges. The center must read 165 °F / 74 °C.
6. Let it rest 10 minutes so it cuts cleanly into 6 squares. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Wrap each square individually in parchment, then put in a freezer bag or container.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven for 30-35 min. Slot it right after the sheet-pan proteins come out (425 °F to 375 °F: let the oven drop 10 minutes). The chicken simmers on the stove before that.

**Fix it:**
- Soggy bottom: the filling was too wet. Simmer the shredded chicken 2 minutes uncovered before layering, and rest the bake 10 minutes before cutting.
- Tortillas tear while layering: warm them 20 seconds in the microwave wrapped in a damp towel.

**Swap:** Swap the chicken for 2 lb of lean ground turkey, browned to 165 °F / 74 °C, or for 3 cups of extra beans plus 1 package of crumbled firm tofu (vegetarian). Use dairy-free cheese for a dairy-free version.
