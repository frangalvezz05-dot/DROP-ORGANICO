### FM10 · Creamy Chicken & Broccoli Pasta Bake
**Makes:** 6 portions (~335 g each) · **Active:** 35 min · **Total:** 60 min · **Oven:** 375 °F / 190 °C
**Per portion:** 530 kcal · 45 g protein · 51 g carbs · 16 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat, milk
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Microwave covered 2-3 min with 1 tbsp milk drizzled on top, stirring once; or bake covered at 350 °F / 175 °C for 15-20 min, until 165 °F / 74 °C in the center.
**Goes with:** V4 Garlic Green Beans, V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad, S12 Creamy Tomato-Garlic (a spoonful on the side)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut in 1 in cubes: 1.5 lb (700 g)
- dry short pasta (penne or rotini): 10 oz (285 g)
- broccoli florets, small: 4 cups (350 g)
- butter: 2 tbsp (28 g)
- all-purpose flour: 3 tbsp (25 g)
- garlic, minced: 3 cloves (9 g)
- 1% milk: 2.5 cups (620 g)
- light cream cheese: 3 oz (6 tbsp) (85 g)
- grated Parmesan: 1/2 cup (2 oz) (50 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)
- black pepper: 1/2 tsp (1 g)
- shredded part-skim mozzarella: 3/4 cup (3 oz) (85 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 375 °F / 190 °C. Boil the pasta in salted water 3 minutes less than the package says (it finishes in the oven). Add the broccoli for the last 2 minutes. Drain; the pasta should be chewy in the center.
2. In a large skillet, cook the chicken in a little of the butter over medium-high heat 5-6 minutes until white inside and 165 °F / 74 °C. Move it to the pasta bowl.
3. In the same skillet melt the rest of the butter over medium. Whisk in the flour and garlic and cook 1 minute. Pour in the milk slowly, whisking, and cook 4-5 minutes until the sauce coats the back of a spoon and a finger drawn across it leaves a clean line.
4. Off the heat, whisk in the cream cheese until smooth, then the Parmesan, salt and pepper.
5. Toss the pasta, broccoli, chicken and sauce together and spread into a 9x13 in / 23x33 cm dish. Sprinkle with the mozzarella. Bake uncovered 20-25 minutes until bubbling at the edges and golden in spots; the center must read 165 °F / 74 °C.
6. Rest 10 minutes, cut into 6 squares. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Wrap squares individually or freeze in flat containers.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven 20-25 min at 375 °F / 190 °C, with the pasta water and sauce on the stove first (minutes 0-30).

**Fix it:**
- Lumpy sauce: the milk went in too fast. Whisk hard off the heat, or push the sauce through a sieve.
- Dry and thick after freezing: stir in 1 tbsp milk per portion when reheating. Cream sauces thicken in the freezer.

**Swap:** Swap the pasta for the same weight of dry whole-wheat pasta or chickpea pasta for more protein. Swap the chicken for 2 cups of canned white beans (vegetarian). Gluten-free: use gluten-free pasta and 3 tbsp cornstarch (instead of flour).
