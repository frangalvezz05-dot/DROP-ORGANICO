### P9 · Crispy Baked Tofu

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 136 g cooked each) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 55 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 340 kcal · 32 g protein · 11 g carbs · 21 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** soy, wheat
**Cost/portion:** about $1.30 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Oven or air fryer, 400 °F / 205 °C for 6-8 minutes to bring back the crisp. Microwave makes it soft.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M6, A2, A7, D-7 · flavour families ASI, MEX, MED

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 blocks (16 oz / 454 g each), extra-firm extra-firm tofu (908 g)
- 3 tbsp cornstarch (24 g)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce (48 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Drain the tofu. Wrap in clean towels, set a heavy pan on top and press 20 minutes.
2. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
3. Tear or cut the tofu into 3/4-inch / 2 cm cubes. Toss gently with the soy sauce and oil, then the garlic powder, paprika and pepper.
4. Sprinkle on the cornstarch and toss until the pieces look dry and lightly coated, with no white clumps.
5. Spread in one layer with space between pieces. Bake 15 minutes, flip, bake 15-18 minutes more.
6. Done when the outside is golden and firm, and a cube sounds hollow when you tap it. It crisps more as it cools. Portion into 5 containers (about 135 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 35 (after a 20-minute press that happens while you prep everything else).
**Fix it:**
- Soft, not crispy: not pressed enough, or the pan was crowded. Pat dry harder and use two pans.
- Stuck to the pan: the parchment was missing or you flipped too soon. Wait until the edge lifts easily.

**Swap:** Gluten-free: use tamari (check the label) instead of soy sauce. Use baked tempeh cubes for more protein per portion, or pressed cubes of extra-firm tofu with 1 tbsp more oil in an air fryer.
