### B5 · Reheat-Ready Pasta

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 150 g cooked each) · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 20 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 280 kcal · 9 g protein · 51 g carbs · 4 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 60-90 sec with 1 tbsp of water or sauce, covered, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).
**Goes with:** signature dishes A8, A10, C3, C7, C10 · flavour families COM, ASI, MED

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 12 oz (340 g) penne or fusilli short dry pasta (penne or fusilli) (340 g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
- 1 tbsp (for the water; only part is absorbed) kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)

**Steps**
1. Bring 3 quarts / 3 L of water to a rolling boil. Add the salt.
2. Add the pasta. Cook 1-2 minutes LESS than the package says. It must still have a firm centre, since it cooks more when you reheat it.
3. Check by biting a piece: you should see a thin white dot in the centre.
4. Drain. Do not rinse. Toss right away with the oil so the pieces do not stick together.
5. Spread on a tray to cool 15 minutes. Portion into 5 containers (about 150 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, minute 0 to 15 while the oven trays roast. Boil the water first thing so it is ready.
**Fix it:**
- Clumped together in the fridge: not enough oil. Add a splash of water on reheat and break up with a fork.
- Mushy after reheating: it was overcooked to start with. Pull it 2 minutes early next time.

**Swap:** Gluten-free: use a corn-and-rice pasta and cook 2 minutes less. Higher protein: use chickpea or lentil pasta (this changes the macros, recalculate).
