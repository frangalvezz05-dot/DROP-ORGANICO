### P8 · Simple Baked Salmon

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 146 g cooked each) · **Active:** 8 min · **Total:** 22 min · **Oven:** 400 °F / 205 °C
**Per portion:** 410 kcal · 37 g protein · 1 g carbs · 27 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** fish
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Eat cold, or microwave at 50% power 60-90 sec. Do not boil it; it dries and smells.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M8, A4, A9, D-3, D-10, C6 · flavour families MEX, ASI, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 lb (skin-off or skin-on) Atlantic salmon fillets (907 g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (15 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (4.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper (0.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 400 °F / 205 °C. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Pat the salmon dry. Cut into 5 even portions (about 180 g raw each).
3. Place on the pan, skin side down. Rub with the oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
4. Bake 11-14 minutes. Done when the thickest part flakes with a fork and reads 145 °F / 63 °C. It should look opaque with a slight shine inside, not dry and chalky.
5. Rest 3 minutes. Cool for up to 1 hour at room temperature, then refrigerate in a sealed container.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 15. Quick tray: put it in last if the oven is busy, but eat it on days 1-2 of the week for best quality.
**Fix it:**
- White liquid on top (albumin): you cooked it hot and fast. Lower to 375 °F and shorten the time next batch.
- Smells fishy on day 4: it is past its best. Plan this protein for the first 2 days and freeze the rest the same day.

**Swap:** Use arctic char or steelhead trout (same weight). For a budget version, use canned salmon (14.75 oz cans) mixed with lemon, but the macros change.
