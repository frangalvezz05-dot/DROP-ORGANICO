### SN1 · Greek Yogurt Protein Cups (3 flavors)
**Makes:** 5 portions (200 g yogurt + toppings; nutrition is the average of the 5 cups) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 10 min
**Per portion:** 280 kcal · 25 g protein · 26 g carbs · 10 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk, peanuts, tree nuts
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (see Reheat) · **Reheat:** Eat cold. Do not freeze. Keep at 40 °F / 4 °C or colder; do not leave out more than 2 hours.
**Goes with:** D3 Greek Yogurt Overnight Oats (same yogurt tub), D4 Peanut Butter-Banana Overnight Oats; stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- plain nonfat Greek yogurt: 4 cups (1000 g)
- strawberries, diced (Berry-Vanilla cups): 1 cup (150 g)
- honey (all flavors): 3 tbsp (63 g)
- vanilla extract (Berry-Vanilla): 1 tsp (4 g)
- unsweetened cocoa powder (Cocoa-Peanut cups): 2 tbsp (11 g)
- smooth peanut butter (Cocoa-Peanut cups): 4 tbsp (64 g)
- ground cinnamon (Cinnamon-Walnut cup): 1/2 tsp (1 g)
- chopped walnuts (Cinnamon-Walnut cup): 2 tbsp (15 g)
- chia seeds (Berry-Vanilla cups): 2 tsp (10 g)

**Steps**
1. Set out 5 jars or containers (about 1 1/4 cups / 300 ml each) with lids. Put 200 g (a heaping 3/4 cup) of yogurt in each. Weighing on a scale is the fastest way to get even cups.
2. Berry-Vanilla (2 cups): stir 2 tsp honey and 1/2 tsp vanilla into the yogurt of each cup until smooth. Top each with 75 g (about 1/2 cup) diced strawberries and 1 tsp chia seeds. The strawberries go on top, not stirred in, so the cup does not turn watery.
3. Cocoa-Peanut (2 cups): stir 1 tbsp cocoa and 1 tsp honey into the yogurt of each cup until there are no streaks. Spoon 2 tbsp peanut butter on top (warm it 10 seconds in the microwave so it spreads).
4. Cinnamon-Walnut (1 cup): stir 1 tbsp honey and the cinnamon into the yogurt. Top with the walnuts.
5. Done when each cup is smooth and thick like pudding with no dry cocoa clumps. Put on the lids and refrigerate right away.
6. Do not freeze: the yogurt separates and turns grainy. Add crunchy toppings (walnuts, granola) just before eating if you want them to stay crisp.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. Assemble in the last 10 minutes of the session, while the oven and stove items rest.

**Fix it:**
- Watery cups: the yogurt wasn't strained. Pour off the liquid on top and stir; next time buy a thick Greek yogurt or strain 10 minutes in a sieve.
- Cocoa clumps: mix the cocoa with the honey first into a paste, then into the yogurt.

**Swap:** Dairy-free: use unsweetened soy yogurt (protein will be lower, about 8-10 g per cup). Nut-free: swap the peanut butter for sunflower seed butter and the walnuts for pumpkin seeds.
