### S9 · Chimichurri
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (40 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 10 min
**Per portion:** 140 kcal · 0 g protein · 2 g carbs · 15 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Thaw in the fridge overnight, or at room temperature 20 min only, then stir.
**Goes with:** D-3, D-5 (Mediterranean plates with S9)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1 cup packed fresh flat-leaf parsley, leaves and tender stems (60 g)
- 3 cloves garlic (9 g)
- 3 Tbsp red wine vinegar (45 g)
- 1/3 cup olive oil (73 g)
- 1 tsp dried oregano (1 g)
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (0.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp fine salt (3 g)

**Steps**
1. Wash the parsley and dry it well. Wet leaves make a watery sauce.
2. Chop the parsley and garlic fine by hand, or pulse 5-6 times in a food processor until it looks like coarse crumbs. Don't purée it.
3. Tip into a bowl with the vinegar, oregano, pepper flakes and salt. Stir and let sit 5 min.
4. Stir in the oil. Taste. It should be sharp and green. Add salt if it tastes flat.
5. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 38 g each). Press a thin film of oil over the top. Refrigerate right away.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 10 minutes. Make it last so it's freshest.

**Fix it:**
- Dull brown-green after a day? That's normal. A squeeze of lemon on serving brings it back.
- Too sharp? Add 1 Tbsp more oil, or reduce the vinegar next time.

**Swap:** Nothing fresh? Use 1 cup packed cilantro for a different but good version. Milder: use 1 garlic clove. Always refrigerate; fresh garlic in oil must not sit at room temperature.
