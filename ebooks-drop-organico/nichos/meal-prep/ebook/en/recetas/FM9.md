### FM9 · Black Bean & Sweet Potato Burritos
**Makes:** 6 burritos (1 per portion, ~350 g each) · **Active:** 30 min · **Total:** 55 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 530 kcal · 24 g protein · 70 g carbs · 18 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat, soy, milk
**Cost/portion:** about $1.85 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Microwave unwrapped, wrapped in a damp paper towel, 1.5-2 min, flipping once; or bake at 375 °F / 190 °C for 20 min. The center must reach 165 °F / 74 °C. Finish in a dry skillet 1 min per side for a crisp outside.
**Goes with:** S1 Chipotle-Lime Yogurt, S2 Tomatillo Salsa Verde, V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- sweet potatoes, peeled and cut in 1/2 in cubes: 1.3 lb (600 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- firm tofu, crumbled: 1 block (16 oz) (454 g)
- chili powder: 1 tbsp (8 g)
- ground cumin: 2 tsp (5 g)
- garlic powder: 1 tsp (3 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)
- red bell pepper, diced: 1 (150 g)
- black beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 2 cans (15 oz) (480 g)
- shredded Mexican-blend cheese: 1 cup (4 oz) (115 g)
- large flour tortillas (10 in): 6 (390 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Toss the sweet potato and pepper with the oil, 1 tsp of the chili powder and half the salt on a sheet pan. Roast 20-25 minutes, stirring once, until a fork goes in easily and the edges are browned.
2. While they roast, press the tofu between paper towels for 5 minutes. Crumble it into a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat with the rest of the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and salt. Cook 6-8 minutes until it looks dry, golden and crumbly like scrambled egg.
3. Mash half the black beans lightly with a fork and leave the rest whole. Mix beans, tofu and roasted vegetables in a big bowl. Taste and add salt if needed. Let the filling cool 10 minutes (hot filling makes the tortilla soggy and steams in the freezer).
4. Warm the tortillas 15 seconds each in the microwave so they fold without cracking. Put about 1 cup filling and 2.5 tbsp cheese in the lower third of each tortilla. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly.
5. Cool completely at room temperature, no longer than 2 hours total. Wrap each burrito in parchment, then foil or a freezer bag. Press out the air.
6. Freeze flat on a tray for 1 hour, then stack. Label with the date.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven for 25 minutes at 425 °F / 220 °C, the same heat as the sheet-pan proteins. Rolling happens at the end, off the stove, while the oven items rest.

**Fix it:**
- Burritos burst open: you overfilled them. Use about 1 cup of filling, and fold in the sides before rolling.
- Soggy after reheating: the filling was warm when rolled, or they sat wrapped warm. Cool them fully, and finish in a dry skillet.

**Swap:** Swap the tofu for 2 cups of cooked shredded chicken (P2 or P3) for a meat version, still 165 °F / 74 °C. Dairy-free: skip the cheese. Gluten-free: use large corn or gluten-free tortillas and warm them well.
