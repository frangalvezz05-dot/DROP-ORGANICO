### SN2 · Hummus & Veg Boxes
**Makes:** 5 portions (60 g hummus + 30 g cheese + 190 g raw veg) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 15 min
**Per portion:** 270 kcal · 14 g protein · 22 g carbs · 16 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** sesame, milk
**Cost/portion:** about $1.65 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (see Reheat) · **Reheat:** Eat cold. Not freezer-friendly (the vegetables turn limp).
**Goes with:** B6 Wraps & Tortillas, S8 Lemon-Tahini (use instead of hummus); stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- hummus, store-bought: 1 1/4 cups (300 g)
- part-skim mozzarella, cut in cubes (or string cheese): 5 oz (150 g)
- cucumber, cut in sticks: 1 large (400 g)
- carrots, cut in sticks: 4 medium (250 g)
- red bell pepper, cut in strips: 2 medium (300 g)
- everything bagel seasoning or paprika (pinch per box): 1 tsp (3 g)

**Steps**
1. Wash and dry all the vegetables completely (wet veg makes the box soggy and shortens its life). Cut cucumber, carrot and pepper into sticks about 3 in / 8 cm long.
2. Spoon 60 g (about 1/4 cup) of hummus into the small compartment of each of 5 containers, or into small lidded cups. Sprinkle a pinch of paprika on top.
3. Divide the cheese cubes (30 g each) and the vegetables (about 190 g each) between the boxes. Wrap the cheese in parchment if you want it fully dry.
4. Done when every box has hummus, cheese and three colors of veg with nothing touching the hummus except the dip cup. Lid and refrigerate.
5. Eat within 4 days. Keep the boxes cold (40 °F / 4 °C or below), especially in a lunch bag; use an ice pack if it will sit more than 2 hours.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 15 minutes, any time; it uses no oven or burner.

**Fix it:**
- Soggy sticks: dry the vegetables with a towel and store the hummus in its own cup.
- Cucumber turns watery by day 4: salt nothing in advance, and swap cucumber for celery after day 3.

**Swap:** Dairy-free: swap the mozzarella for 1/2 cup of roasted chickpeas (SN5) per box. Sesame-free: use a hummus without tahini or mashed white beans with lemon and garlic.
