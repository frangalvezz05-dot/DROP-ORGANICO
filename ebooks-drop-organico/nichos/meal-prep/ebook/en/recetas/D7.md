### D7 · Freezer Protein Pancakes
**Makes:** 5 portions (3 pancakes, about 150 g) · **Active:** 30 min · **Total:** 40 min
**Per portion:** 230 kcal · 16 g protein · 22 g carbs · 9 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, milk
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Toaster or toaster oven (375 °F / 190 °C) 4-5 min until hot and crisp at the edges. Or microwave 3 pancakes 45-60 sec. From frozen: add 1 min.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Syrup, peanut butter or fruit are extra and not counted.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats (100 g)
- 1 1/2 cups 2% cottage cheese (340 g)
- 4 large eggs (200 g)
- 1 small ripe banana (100 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder (7 g)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (4.5 g)
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon (2.6 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)
- 2 tsp canola oil (for the pan) (9 g)

**Steps**
1. Blend the oats, cottage cheese, eggs, banana, baking powder, vanilla, cinnamon and salt in a blender 30-40 sec until smooth. Rest the batter 5 min so the oats soften.
2. Heat a nonstick pan or griddle over medium heat. Brush with a little oil.
3. Pour 3 Tbsp / 45 g of batter per pancake, about 4 inches wide. Cook 2-3 min until bubbles form and pop on top and the edges look dry.
4. Flip. Cook 1-2 min until golden underneath and the center springs back (160 °F / 71 °C). Re-oil the pan every batch. Make 15 pancakes.
5. Cool the pancakes in a single layer on a rack, 10 min. Stacked warm pancakes steam and stick.
6. Pack 3 per portion. For the freezer, freeze flat on a tray 1 hour, then bag them with parchment between layers.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire. One pan, about 25 min of active time. Do it while the oven bakes everything else.

**Fix it:**
- Pancakes tear when flipped? Wait for bubbles to pop across the surface and the edges to look dry, and use a thin spatula.
- Raw center? The heat is too high. Drop to medium-low and make them smaller.

**Swap:** Gluten-free: use certified gluten-free oats. More protein: replace 2 Tbsp of the oats with 2 Tbsp unflavored or vanilla protein powder (macros change). No banana: use 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce.
