### SN5 · Crunchy Roasted Chickpeas
**Makes:** 5 portions (about 100 g roasted (3/4 cup)) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 50 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 230 kcal · 10 g protein · 34 g carbs · 7 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $0.50 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 5 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Re-crisp in a 400 °F / 200 °C oven or air fryer for 5 minutes. Counter: 2 days in a loosely closed jar. Fridge: 5 days, but they soften. Freezer: 3 months in a bag; re-crisp from frozen.
**Goes with:** P10 Smoky Spiced Chickpeas (a bigger, saucier version), V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad (as a crunchy topper); stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed: 3 cans (15 oz) (720 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- smoked paprika: 2 tsp (5 g)
- garlic powder: 1 tsp (3 g)
- ground cumin: 1 tsp (2 g)
- kosher salt: 3/4 tsp (4 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Dry the chickpeas very well: roll them in a clean towel and rub off the loose skins. Dry chickpeas turn crisp; wet ones steam and stay soft.
2. Spread them on two sheet pans (don't crowd them). Toss with the oil until lightly glossy.
3. Roast 25-30 minutes, shaking the pans every 10 minutes. They are done when they rattle on the pan, look deep golden, and one cooled chickpea crunches when bitten.
4. Right out of the oven, toss with the paprika, garlic powder, cumin and salt so the spices stick to the hot oil.
5. Cool fully on the pans (15 minutes) before storing, or steam in the container will make them soft. Divide into 5 portions of about 3/4 cup / 100 g.
6. Store in a container that is not fully sealed for the first day, then close it. They are crispest the first 2 days.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven at 425 °F / 220 °C for 25-30 min on the second rack. Same heat as the sheet-pan proteins, so they ride along.

**Fix it:**
- Chewy, not crisp: not dry enough or the pan was crowded. Dry them again, use two pans, and roast 8 minutes longer.
- Burnt on the outside, soft inside: the oven runs hot. Drop to 400 °F / 200 °C and roast 5 minutes longer.

**Swap:** Sweet version: skip the paprika, garlic and cumin; toss with 1 tsp cinnamon and 1 tbsp honey after roasting. Swap the chickpeas for canned white beans (they roast softer).
