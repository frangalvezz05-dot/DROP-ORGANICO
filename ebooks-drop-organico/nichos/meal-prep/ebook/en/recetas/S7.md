### S7 · Tzatziki
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (45 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 8 min · **Total:** 8 min
**Per portion:** 40 kcal · 3 g protein · 2 g carbs · 2 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Cost/portion:** about $0.30 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Serve cold. Don't heat. A little water on top is normal; stir it back in.
**Goes with:** D-1, D-4, D-9, D-10, D-12 (Mediterranean plates with S7)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2/3 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt (150 g)
- 1/4 cup cucumber, grated and squeezed dry (40 g)
- 2 tsp lemon juice (10 g)
- 2 tsp olive oil (9 g)
- 1 small clove garlic, grated (3 g)
- 1/2 tsp dried dill (0.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Grate the cucumber on the big holes. Wrap it in a clean towel or paper towel and squeeze hard until only a damp clump is left.
2. Stir the yogurt, lemon juice, oil, grated garlic, dill and salt in a bowl until smooth.
3. Fold in the cucumber.
4. Taste. Add salt until the cucumber tastes sweet, not watery. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 43 g each). Chill 30 min.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 8 minutes any time. Do it first so the flavors blend.

**Fix it:**
- Watery next day? The cucumber wasn't squeezed enough. Spoon off the liquid and stir in 1 Tbsp thick yogurt.
- Garlic too sharp? Let it sit 1 hour, or use half a clove.

**Swap:** No dairy: unsweetened plain soy or coconut yogurt. Fresh dill: use 1 Tbsp chopped in place of dried.
