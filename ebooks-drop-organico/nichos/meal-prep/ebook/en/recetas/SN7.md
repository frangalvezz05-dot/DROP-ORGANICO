### SN7 · Cottage Cheese Ranch Dip
**Makes:** 5 portions (1/2 cup dip (120 g) + about 150 g raw veg) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 10 min
**Per portion:** 130 kcal · 13 g protein · 14 g carbs · 2 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Fridge:** 5 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (see Reheat) · **Reheat:** Eat cold. Do not freeze: it splits and turns grainy.
**Goes with:** V5 Sesame Cabbage-Carrot Slaw, B6 Wraps & Tortillas (as a spread), P3 Never-Dry Baked Chicken Breast (as a dip for sliced chicken); stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2% cottage cheese: 2 cups (450 g)
- plain nonfat Greek yogurt: 1/2 cup (125 g)
- lemon juice: 1 tbsp (15 g)
- garlic powder: 1/2 tsp (2 g)
- onion powder: 1/2 tsp (2 g)
- dried dill: 1 tsp (1 g)
- fresh parsley, finely chopped: 2 tbsp (8 g)
- kosher salt: 1/2 tsp (3 g)
- baby carrots: 10 oz (300 g)
- celery sticks: 5 stalks (250 g)
- cucumber, in rounds: 1 large (200 g)

**Steps**
1. Put the cottage cheese, yogurt, lemon juice, garlic powder, onion powder and dill in a blender or food processor.
2. Blend 45-60 seconds, scraping down once, until completely smooth and pourable like thick sour cream, with no visible curds.
3. Stir in the parsley and salt by hand. Taste: it should be tangy and savory. Add a pinch more salt or a few drops of lemon if needed.
4. Spoon 1/2 cup (120 g) of dip into each of 5 small lidded containers or jars. Chill at least 1 hour; it thickens as it chills.
5. Cut the veg (carrots, celery, cucumber) and divide into the 5 boxes, about 150 g each. Keep the dip and the veg in separate compartments.
6. Eat within 5 days. Stir before eating since a little liquid may rise to the top.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 10 minutes, any time. A blender and nothing else.

**Fix it:**
- Dip too thin: strain the cottage cheese 10 minutes before blending, or stir in 2 tbsp more yogurt.
- Tastes bland: it needs salt and acid. Add salt in pinches and a squeeze of lemon, then rest it 30 minutes so the flavor develops.

**Swap:** Lactose-light: use lactose-free cottage cheese. Spicy: add 1 tsp hot sauce. Swap the veg for whole-wheat pita triangles or the baked chickpeas from SN5.
