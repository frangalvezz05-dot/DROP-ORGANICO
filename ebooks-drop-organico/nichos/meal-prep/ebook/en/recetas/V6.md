### V6 · Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad (raw)

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 180 g cooked each) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 15 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 80 kcal · 2 g protein · 8 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Not reheated. Eat cold. Add the dressing on the day.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M2, M7, D-1, D-4, D-6, D-10, D-12 · flavour families MED, MEX

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 medium, diced cucumbers (400 g)
- 1 lb Roma or cherry tomatoes, diced tomatoes (Roma or cherry) (400 g)
- 1/2 small, finely diced red onion (80 g)
- 1/4 cup, chopped fresh parsley (15 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 2 tbsp lemon juice (30 g)
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano (0.8 g)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (4.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Dice the cucumbers and tomatoes into 1/2-inch pieces. Scoop out the seedy centre of the tomatoes if they are very juicy.
2. Finely dice the red onion. Soak it in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain, to take away the sharp bite.
3. Mix the cucumber, tomato, onion and parsley in a bowl. Do not add the salt yet.
4. In a jar, shake the oil, lemon juice, oregano and salt until creamy.
5. Portion the salad into 5 containers (about 180 g each) lined with a paper towel, with the dressing in a small cup.
6. On eating day, pour the dressing over. It should taste bright and a little salty, with crisp cucumber.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold prep, no heat. Prep it last, as it is the most perishable thing in the week.
**Fix it:**
- Soggy: salt and dressing draw out water. Dress only at the last minute.
- Bland tomatoes: add a pinch of sugar and extra lemon. Cherry tomatoes hold up better than big ones.

**Swap:** Add 1/2 cup crumbled feta or a can of chickpeas for a heartier salad (macros change). Use bell pepper if cucumbers are out of season.
