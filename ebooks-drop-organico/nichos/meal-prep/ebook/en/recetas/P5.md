### P5 · Beef & Lentil Power Mince

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 160 g cooked each) · **Active:** 20 min · **Total:** 50 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 330 kcal · 27 g protein · 27 g carbs · 13 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $2.10 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 90 sec, stirring halfway, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).
**Goes with:** signature dishes M4, M12, D-6, C5, C10 · flavour families MEX, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1 lb 90% lean ground beef (454 g)
- 3/4 cup (150 g) dry brown lentils (150 g)
- 1 medium, diced yellow onion (150 g)
- 1 large, finely diced carrots (100 g)
- 3 cloves, minced garlic cloves (9 g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste (32 g)
- 2 tsp ground cumin (4 g)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika (5 g)
- 1 tsp chili powder (2.7 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (9 g)

**Steps**
1. Rinse the lentils in a sieve and pick out any stones.
2. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high. Add the beef and break it up. Cook 6-8 minutes until browned and no pink is left (160 °F / 71 °C).
3. Add the onion and carrot. Cook 5 minutes until the onion is soft.
4. Add the garlic, tomato paste and spices. Stir 1 minute until the paste darkens a shade.
5. Add the lentils, the salt and 3 cups water. Bring to a boil, then lower to a steady simmer, partly covered.
6. Cook 22-28 minutes, stirring now and then. Done when the lentils are tender but not mushy and the pot is thick like chili, with almost no loose liquid. Add water 1/4 cup at a time if it dries too soon.
7. Cool, then portion into 5 containers (about 160 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, minute 0 to 45, one pot. Lentils simmer while you chop the rest. Lowest-cost protein of the book: 1 lb of beef covers 5 portions.
**Fix it:**
- Hard lentils after 30 minutes: old lentils. Add 1/2 cup water and simmer 10 more minutes.
- Too wet: simmer uncovered 5 minutes. Too thick on day 3: loosen with a splash of water.

**Swap:** Use ground turkey in place of beef, or half the beef and a second 1/2 cup of lentils for the cheapest week. Red lentils cook in 12 minutes but go soft.
