### P6 · Freezer Turkey Meatballs

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 156 g cooked each) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 35 min · **Oven:** 400 °F / 205 °C
**Per portion:** 360 kcal · 39 g protein · 10 g carbs · 18 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk, egg, wheat
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 6 meatballs 60-90 sec, or 12 min at 350 °F / 175 °C from frozen, until 165 °F / 74 °C in the centre.
**Goes with:** signature dishes A6, A10, D-5, D-12, C3, C12 · flavour families MED, COM, ASI

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 lb 93% lean ground turkey (907 g)
- 1 large egg (50 g)
- 1/2 cup plain dry breadcrumbs (55 g)
- 1/4 cup (about 1 oz) grated Parmesan (28 g)
- 3 cloves, minced garlic cloves (9 g)
- 1 tsp onion powder (2.5 g)
- 1 tsp dried oregano (1.5 g)
- 2 tbsp, chopped fresh parsley (8 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (9 g)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 400 °F / 205 °C. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
2. In a large bowl, mix the egg, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, garlic, onion powder, oregano, parsley, salt and pepper. Let it sit 3 minutes so the crumbs soak up the egg.
3. Add the turkey. Mix with your hands only until it just comes together. Overmixing makes them tough.
4. Scoop 30 even balls (about 2 tbsp each, a bit over 1 oz / 35 g) and roll them smooth. Wet your hands if it sticks.
5. Place on the pan, close but not touching. Bake 18-20 minutes. Done when browned on top and a thermometer in the middle reads 165 °F / 74 °C.
6. Cool 15 minutes. Portion 6 meatballs per container (about 155 g). To freeze, lay on a tray for 1 hour, then bag.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 25. Freezer champion: double the batch and freeze the second half flat on a tray, then bag.
**Fix it:**
- Dry meatballs: you overmixed, or baked past 165 °F. Reheat them in a covered pan with a spoonful of sauce.
- Falling apart: the mix was too wet. Add 1 more tbsp breadcrumbs and chill 10 minutes.

**Swap:** Gluten-free: swap the breadcrumbs for 1/2 cup rolled oats pulsed fine. Dairy-free: leave out the Parmesan and add 1/4 tsp more salt. Ground chicken works too.
