### FM6 · Chicken Tortilla Soup
**Makes:** 6 portions (~655 g each) · **Active:** 20 min · **Total:** 50 min
**Per portion:** 560 kcal · 58 g protein · 43 g carbs · 19 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Fridge:** 3 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Reheat in a pot over medium 6-8 min or microwave covered 3-4 min, until 165 °F / 74 °C in the center. Add toppings after heating.
**Goes with:** B1 Hands-Off Oven Rice (a scoop in the bowl), S1 Chipotle-Lime Yogurt, V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- boneless skinless chicken breasts: 2.2 lb (1000 g)
- olive oil: 1 tbsp (14 g)
- yellow onion, diced: 1 large (200 g)
- jalapeño, seeded and minced: 1 (30 g)
- garlic, minced: 4 cloves (12 g)
- chili powder: 1 tbsp (8 g)
- ground cumin: 2 tsp (5 g)
- diced tomatoes with green chilies: 1 can (14.5 oz) (411 g)
- low-sodium chicken broth: 6 cups (1440 g)
- black beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- pinto beans, canned, drained and rinsed: 1 can (15 oz) (240 g)
- frozen corn kernels: 1 cup (150 g)
- lime juice: 3 tbsp (45 g)
- fresh cilantro, chopped: 1/2 cup (15 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)
- corn tortillas, cut in strips (topping, baked fresh on serving day): 6 (156 g)
- shredded Monterey Jack (1 oz per portion): 6 oz (170 g)

_Nutrition note: includes the tortilla strips and 28 g of cheese topping_

**Steps**
1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Cook the onion and jalapeño 5 minutes until soft. Add garlic, chili powder and cumin for 30 seconds.
2. Add the tomatoes with chilies and the broth. Lay the whole chicken breasts in the pot. Bring to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook 18-20 minutes until the thickest breast reads 165 °F / 74 °C and is white inside.
3. Move the chicken to a board and shred with two forks. Return it to the pot with both beans and the corn. Simmer 5 more minutes.
4. Off the heat, stir in the lime juice, cilantro and salt. Taste: it should taste bright, not flat.
5. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
6. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Freeze the soup only. The tortilla strips and cheese are added on serving day.
7. Serving-day topping (macros include it): toss the strips with a little spray oil, bake at 400 °F / 200 °C for 8-10 minutes until crisp and light golden. Top each bowl with the strips and 1 oz / 28 g cheese.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop. Start at minute 15; 35 minutes of mostly waiting. The tortilla strips go in the oven for 10 minutes on the day you eat it, not on Sunday.

**Fix it:**
- Chicken stringy and dry: it boiled hard. Keep the simmer lazy and pull the breasts out the minute they hit 165 °F / 74 °C.
- Soup tastes like plain broth: add salt a pinch at a time and another splash of lime juice at the end, never before the simmer.

**Swap:** Swap chicken breast for thighs (same weight) for juicier meat; they need about 25 minutes. Dairy-free: skip the cheese and top with sliced avocado on serving day.
