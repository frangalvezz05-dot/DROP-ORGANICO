### B6 · Wraps & Tortillas (Store & Assemble)

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 50 g cooked each) · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 5 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 150 kcal · 4 g protein · 25 g carbs · 4 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat
**Cost/portion:** about $0.35 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 7 days (opened package) · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Warm 15 seconds per side in a dry pan, or 10 seconds in the microwave wrapped in a damp towel. Assemble the wrap at lunch.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M4, M7, M11, D-4, D-9, D-12, C8, C9 · flavour families MEX, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 5 flour tortillas, 8-inch flour tortillas (8-inch) (250 g)

**Steps**
1. Check the date on the package. Keep the tortillas in the fridge once opened.
2. Stack the tortillas in two groups, with a square of parchment between each.
3. Slide them into a zip bag and press out the air. For 5 days, one bag is enough.
4. To freeze beyond a week: wrap in foil, then a freezer bag. Defrost overnight in the fridge.
5. Assemble the wrap on the day. Warm the tortilla, then fill with 1 protein portion, a vegetable portion and a spoonful of sauce, and fold in the sides before rolling.
6. For a lunch you take with you: pack the filling in a container and the tortilla in a bag, and assemble at lunch to avoid sogginess.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** No cooking at all. Minute 0: portion and wrap. Do this while the oven works.
**Fix it:**
- Cracks when you fold it: it is cold or dry. Warm 15 seconds in a dry pan to make it bendable.
- Soggy by lunch: the filling was wet. Keep sauce in its own cup and add it at the last minute.

**Swap:** Use corn tortillas (6-inch, 2 per portion) for gluten-free tacos, or low-carb wraps, but check the label. Check macros for any wrap that is not plain flour.
