### D6 · Cottage Cheese Baked Oats
**Makes:** 5 portions (1 large square, about 255 g) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 60 min · **Oven:** 350 °F / 175 °C
**Per portion:** 370 kcal · 22 g protein · 50 g carbs · 10 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg, milk
**Cost/portion:** about $1.90 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Microwave a square 60-90 sec until hot in the middle. Or 350 °F / 175 °C oven 10 min, covered. Good cold too.
**Goes with:** Breakfast slot. Tastes like dessert. Cut into 5 on Sunday and it reheats well all week.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats (170 g)
- 2 cups 2% cottage cheese (450 g)
- 4 large eggs (200 g)
- 2 medium ripe bananas (236 g)
- 1/2 cup 1% milk (122 g)
- 2 Tbsp pure maple syrup (40 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder (7 g)
- 2 tsp vanilla extract (9 g)
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon (5 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)
- 1 cup blueberries (fresh or frozen) (150 g)
- 1 tsp cooking spray or butter (for the dish) (4.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 350 °F / 175 °C. Grease a 9x13 inch / 23x33 cm baking dish.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, bananas, milk, maple syrup, vanilla, baking powder, cinnamon and salt in a blender until smooth and pourable, about 30 sec.
3. Stir in the oats in a large bowl until all the oats are wet.
4. Pour into the dish and spread level. Scatter the blueberries over the top, pressing in lightly.
5. Bake 40-45 min until the edges are golden, the middle doesn't jiggle, and a knife in the center comes out clean (160 °F / 71 °C).
6. Cool 15 min. Cut into 5 squares (or more, thinner). Cool completely before packing.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, 45 min at 350 °F. Put it on the lower rack beside the main tray or in first thing.

**Fix it:**
- Wet in the middle? Bake 5-8 min more. Cover the top with foil if it browns too fast.
- Dry or rubbery edges? Your oven runs hot or the dish was smaller than 9x13. Check at 35 min next time.

**Swap:** Cheaper: use frozen berries. Dairy-free: not a good fit (cottage cheese is the structure); try D3 with plant yogurt. Less sweet: use 1 Tbsp maple syrup.
