### B4 · Roasted Sweet Potato Cubes

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 170 g cooked each) · **Active:** 15 min · **Total:** 45 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 220 kcal · 3 g protein · 41 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $0.60 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 60-90 sec, covered, or 8 minutes in a 400 °F / 205 °C oven to re-crisp.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M3, A5, A11, D-8, C2, C12 · flavour families MEX, ASI, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2.2 lb (about 3 medium), peeled, 3/4-inch cubes sweet potatoes (1000 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Line a pan with parchment.
2. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut into 3/4-inch / 2 cm cubes. Keep them the same size so they cook evenly.
3. Toss with the oil, salt, paprika and pepper. Spread in one layer, not touching.
4. Roast 15 minutes, flip, then roast 12-15 minutes more.
5. Done when the edges are browned and caramelised, and a fork slides into the centre with no resistance.
6. Cool, then portion into 5 containers (about 170 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 35. Needs one flip. Works on the same rack as other 425 °F trays.
**Fix it:**
- Soft and no browning: the pan was crowded. Use two pans or reduce to a single layer.
- Burnt edges, hard middle: cubes too large. Next time cut to 3/4 inch.

**Swap:** Butternut squash cubes work in the same time. For a sweeter finish, add 1/2 tsp cinnamon in place of the paprika.
