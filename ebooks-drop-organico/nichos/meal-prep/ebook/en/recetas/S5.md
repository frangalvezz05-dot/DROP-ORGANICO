### S5 · Peanut-Ginger Sauce
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (35 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 5 min
**Per portion:** 100 kcal · 3 g protein · 7 g carbs · 7 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** peanuts, soy, wheat, sesame
**Fridge:** 5 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Serve cool or room temperature. Thin with 1 tsp warm water if it has set firm.
**Goes with:** A2, A5, A8, A12 (Asian plates with S5)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1/4 cup smooth peanut butter (no sugar added) (65 g)
- 1 Tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce (16 g)
- 1 Tbsp lime juice (15 g)
- 1 Tbsp honey (21 g)
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely grated (2 g)
- 1 clove garlic, grated (3 g)
- 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (2 g)
- 1/2 tsp sriracha (3 g)
- warm, 1/4 cup water (60 g)

**Steps**
1. Put the peanut butter in a bowl. Add soy sauce, lime juice, honey, ginger, garlic, sesame oil and sriracha.
2. Whisk it. It will look thick and grainy at first. Keep going.
3. Whisk in the warm water 1 Tbsp at a time. Stop when it falls from the whisk in a smooth ribbon.
4. Taste. More lime if it's heavy, more soy sauce if it's flat.
5. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 37 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 5 minutes anywhere in the session. No heat.

**Fix it:**
- Split or oily? Your peanut butter was cold or natural-style with a layer of oil. Whisk in 1 Tbsp warm water.
- Too thick after chilling? Whisk in 1 tsp warm water per portion when you serve it.

**Swap:** Peanut allergy: use sunflower seed butter (nut-free; macros change). Spicier: double the sriracha.
