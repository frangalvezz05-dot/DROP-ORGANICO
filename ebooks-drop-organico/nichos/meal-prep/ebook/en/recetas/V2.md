### V2 · Sheet-Pan Fajita Peppers & Onions

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 130 g cooked each) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 35 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 110 kcal · 2 g protein · 14 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 60 sec, or a hot dry pan for 3 minutes for better texture.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M1, M3, M6, M11, C5, C8 · flavour families MEX

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 3 bell peppers (red, green or mix), sliced bell peppers (red and green) (600 g)
- 2 medium, sliced in 1/2-inch strips yellow onion (300 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 2 tsp chili powder (5 g)
- 1 tsp ground cumin (2 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 1 tbsp lime juice (15 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C.
2. Slice the peppers and onions into strips about 1/2 inch / 1 cm wide, all the same size.
3. Toss with the oil, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and salt.
4. Spread over a large pan in one layer. Do not crowd.
5. Roast 25-28 minutes, stirring once at 15 minutes. Done when the onions are soft and golden at the edges and the peppers have blistered spots.
6. Squeeze the lime juice on top. Portion into 5 containers (about 130 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 28 at 425 °F / 220 °C. Shares the oven with P1, P3, P9 and P10.
**Fix it:**
- Watery: the pan was too crowded. Use 2 pans or drain the liquid before storing.
- Too crunchy: slice them thinner next time and leave 5 more minutes.

**Swap:** Add 1 sliced poblano or a jalapeno for heat. If peppers are expensive, use half peppers and half zucchini.
