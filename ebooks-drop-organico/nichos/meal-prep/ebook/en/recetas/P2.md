### P2 · Slow-Cooker Salsa Shredded Chicken

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 140 g cooked each) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 250 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 270 kcal · 46 g protein · 7 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $2.45 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 90 sec with 1 tbsp of its cooking juices, stir halfway, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).
**Goes with:** signature dishes M2, M9, M11, C8 · flavour families MEX, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2.2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (1000 g)
- 1 1/2 cups (1 jar, 16 oz) jarred salsa (medium) (360 g)
- 2 tsp ground cumin (4 g)
- 2 tsp chili powder (5 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 2 tbsp lime juice (30 g)

**Steps**
1. Lay the chicken breasts in the slow cooker in one layer if you can. Sprinkle with cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and salt.
2. Pour the salsa over the top. Do not add water: the chicken gives off its own liquid.
3. Cover. Cook on LOW 6-7 hours or HIGH 3-4 hours. Done when a thermometer in the thickest breast reads 165 °F / 74 °C and a fork shreds it with no effort.
4. Move the chicken to a bowl. Shred with two forks or by beating it 20 seconds with a hand mixer.
5. Stir in the lime juice and 1/2 cup of the cooking liquid. Taste and add a pinch of salt if needed.
6. Portion into 5 containers (about 140 g each) with a spoonful of juices on each so it stays moist.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Slow cooker, minute 0. It works alone for 3-4 hours on HIGH, so start it before anything else. It never touches the oven or the stove.
**Fix it:**
- Watery? Simmer the leftover liquid 5 minutes in a pan, or just use less when mixing back in.
- Stringy and dry: it cooked too long on HIGH. Next time check at 3 hours, and always mix the juices back in.

**Swap:** Use boneless thighs (same weight, shred more tender) or a green salsa (tomatillo) for a milder, tangy version. Low on money: bone-in breasts, 3 lb, shredded after cooking.
