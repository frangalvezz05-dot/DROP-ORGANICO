### P3 · Never-Dry Baked Chicken Breast

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 150 g cooked each) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 45 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 270 kcal · 45 g protein · 1 g carbs · 8 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Cost/portion:** about $1.90 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave at 70% power for 60-90 sec, or slice and warm in a covered pan with a splash of water. Never full power for long.
**Goes with:** signature dishes A5, A11, D-4, D-11, C4, C9 · flavour families MEX, ASI, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2.2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (1000 g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Salt the chicken (the 1 tsp) on both sides 15-30 minutes before cooking, or overnight in the fridge. This is a dry brine.
2. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Place a chicken breast between plastic wrap or in a bag and pound the thick end with a pan or rolling pin until it is an even 3/4 inch / 2 cm thick. Do all breasts.
3. Rub with the oil, then the pepper, garlic powder and paprika. Lay on a lined sheet pan, with space between them.
4. Bake 16-20 minutes. Start checking at 14. Done when a thermometer in the thickest part reads 160 °F / 71 °C, then it carries up to 165 °F / 74 °C as it rests.
5. Rest covered with foil for 5-10 minutes. Cutting early lets the juices run out.
6. Slice against the grain. Portion into 5 containers (150 g each) and drizzle any pan juices on top.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 25. Can share the oven with a veg tray at the same 425 °F / 220 °C.
**Fix it:**
- Rubbery or dry: it went past 165 °F or the breasts were uneven. Pound them flat next time and use the thermometer, not the clock.
- White and wet on the surface: pat the chicken dry before the oil goes on.

**Swap:** Use thin-sliced breast cutlets (bake 10-12 minutes) to save pounding. Swap the spices for 2 tsp Italian seasoning or taco seasoning for a different family.
