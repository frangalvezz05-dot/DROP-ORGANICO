### S11 · Honey-Mustard Yogurt
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (45 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 5 min
**Per portion:** 40 kcal · 3 g protein · 7 g carbs · 0 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Serve cold. Don't heat.
**Goes with:** C4, C6, C9, C12 (Comfort plates with S11)

*Macro note:* Costed with yellow mustard entry; Dijon is similar.

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2/3 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt (150 g)
- 2 Tbsp Dijon or yellow mustard (30 g)
- 1 1/2 Tbsp honey (32 g)
- 1 tsp lemon juice (5 g)
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder (0.8 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)
- a pinch black pepper (0.3 g)

**Steps**
1. Put the yogurt in a bowl.
2. Add the mustard, honey, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
3. Stir until smooth and pale gold, with no streaks of mustard or honey.
4. Taste. More honey if it's sharp, more mustard if it's sweet.
5. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 44 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Cold. 5 minutes anywhere. No heat.

**Fix it:**
- Too thin? Strain the yogurt next time, or stir in 1 more Tbsp yogurt.
- Too sweet or too sharp? Balance with a little honey or a little lemon.

**Swap:** No dairy: unsweetened plain soy or coconut yogurt. Less sugar: use 1 Tbsp honey.
