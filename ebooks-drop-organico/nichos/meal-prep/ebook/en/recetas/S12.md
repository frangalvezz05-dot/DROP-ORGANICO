### S12 · Creamy Tomato-Garlic Sauce
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (45 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 15 min
**Per portion:** 60 kcal · 2 g protein · 4 g carbs · 5 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** milk
**Cost/portion:** about $0.35 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Warm in a pan over low heat, whisking, or microwave at 50% power 45 sec. Stir.
**Goes with:** C3, C7, C10 (Comfort plates with S12)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1 Tbsp olive oil (13.5 g)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (9 g)
- 3/4 cup tomato sauce (plain, canned) (184 g)
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano (0.5 g)
- 3 Tbsp reduced-fat cream cheese, cubed (45 g)
- 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan (10 g)
- 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)

**Steps**
1. Warm the oil in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic. Cook 30-60 sec, stirring, until fragrant and just pale gold. Don't let it brown.
2. Pour in the tomato sauce and add the oregano and salt. Simmer on low 8-10 min, stirring now and then, until it darkens slightly and thickens.
3. Turn the heat to the lowest setting. Add the cream cheese and whisk until fully melted and the sauce turns a smooth, creamy pink-orange.
4. Whisk in the Parmesan off the heat. Taste and add salt if it needs it.
5. Cool 10 min. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 45 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire. One burner, 15 min. Low heat after the first 2 minutes.

**Fix it:**
- Grainy or split? The heat was too high. Whisk in 1 Tbsp warm water off the heat.
- Too acidic? Stir in 1/4 tsp sugar or honey.

**Swap:** No dairy: use a plant-based cream cheese and skip the Parmesan (macros change). Lighter: skip the cream cheese and use 3 Tbsp Parmesan.
