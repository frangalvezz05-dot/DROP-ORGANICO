### P10 · Smoky Spiced Chickpeas

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 140 g cooked each) · **Active:** 8 min · **Total:** 35 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 260 kcal · 11 g protein · 36 g carbs · 9 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 2 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 60 sec, or 5 minutes in a hot oven or air fryer to bring back the edges.
**Goes with:** signature dishes M7, D-2, D-9 · flavour families MED, MEX

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 3 cans (15 oz), drained and rinsed canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed (750 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika (5 g)
- 1 tsp ground cumin (2 g)
- 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)
- 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (15 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C.
2. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Spread on a towel and pat very dry. Dry beans roast. Wet beans steam.
3. Toss on a sheet pan with the oil, paprika, cumin, garlic powder and salt.
4. Roast 25-30 minutes, shaking the pan once at the halfway point. Done when the chickpeas are golden, shrunken a little and the skins split.
5. Toss with the lemon juice right out of the oven.
6. Cool, then portion into 5 containers (about 140 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 30. Cheapest protein in the book, and the only plant protein P with no pressing or soaking.
**Fix it:**
- Soft in the fridge: normal, they take on moisture. Re-crisp them 5 minutes in the oven or air fryer.
- Burnt spice on the pan: the oven was too hot or the pan was dark. Lower to 400 °F / 205 °C and use parchment.

**Swap:** Use white beans or black beans (canned, rinsed). Skip the smoke with 2 tsp Italian seasoning for a Mediterranean version. Each portion has about 11 g of protein, about a third of the chicken recipes: pair it with a higher-protein sauce like Greek yogurt.
