### P4 · Turkey Taco Crumble

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 140 g cooked each) · **Active:** 20 min · **Total:** 25 min · **Oven:** none
**Per portion:** 330 kcal · 35 g protein · 7 g carbs · 19 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Microwave 90 sec, stirring halfway, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).
**Goes with:** signature dishes M3, M10 · flavour families MEX

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 lb 93% lean ground turkey (907 g)
- 1 medium, finely diced yellow onion (150 g)
- 3 cloves, minced garlic cloves (9 g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste (32 g)
- 2 tbsp chili powder (16 g)
- 2 tsp ground cumin (4 g)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)
- 1 tsp dried oregano (1.5 g)
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (9 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and a pinch of salt. Cook 4-5 minutes until soft and see-through.
2. Add the garlic, chili powder, cumin, paprika and oregano. Stir 30 seconds until it smells toasty.
3. Add the turkey. Break it up with a wooden spoon or a potato masher. Cook 7-9 minutes until no pink is left and it reaches 165 °F / 74 °C (ground turkey is safe at 165 °F).
4. Stir in the tomato paste, the salt and 1/2 cup water. Simmer 3-4 minutes until the liquid has mostly cooked off and the meat looks glossy, not soupy.
5. Cool for 20 minutes, spread out in a wide dish. Portion into 5 containers (about 140 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, one large skillet, minute 0 to 20. Frees the pan for a second batch of anything.
**Fix it:**
- Dry and crumbly: add 2-3 tbsp water and simmer 1 minute, then do the same when you reheat.
- Bland: more salt and a squeeze of lime at reheat. Turkey needs more salt than beef.

**Swap:** Use 93% lean ground chicken (same weight) or ground beef (90%, adds fat and a little protein). Out of chili powder: 1 tbsp taco seasoning replaces the three spices.
