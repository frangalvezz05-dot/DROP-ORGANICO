### SN3 · Perfect Hard-Boiled Eggs
**Makes:** 5 portions (2 large eggs (about 100 g peeled)) · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 25 min
**Per portion:** 160 kcal · 13 g protein · 1 g carbs · 11 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** egg
**Cost/portion:** about $0.40 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 7 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (see Reheat) · **Reheat:** Eat cold. Do not microwave in the shell (they can burst). Do not freeze: the whites turn rubbery.
**Goes with:** D1 Spinach-Feta Egg Bites (same protein, different style), V5 Sesame Cabbage-Carrot Slaw; stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- large eggs, cold from the fridge: 10 (500 g)
- kosher salt (pinch per portion): 1/2 tsp (3 g)
- smoked paprika or everything seasoning (optional, pinch per portion): 1/2 tsp (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Put the eggs in a single layer in a large pot. Cover with cold water by 1 in / 2.5 cm. Bring to a full rolling boil over high heat.
2. As soon as the water boils, cover the pot, turn off the heat, and slide it off the burner. Set a timer for 12 minutes.
3. While they sit, fill a big bowl with ice and water. When the timer rings, move the eggs into the ice bath for 10 minutes. Cold shock stops the cooking and makes them easy to peel.
4. Peel under a trickle of cool water, starting at the wide end where the air pocket is. A done egg has a fully set, pale yellow yolk with no green ring and no wet center.
5. Keep the eggs in their shells in a lidded container for up to 7 days; peeled eggs keep best in a container with a damp paper towel and are best within 5 days. Divide into 5 portions of 2 eggs. Add the salt and paprika when you eat them.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, minute 0. It is 12 minutes of waiting, so start it first and forget it.

**Fix it:**
- Green-gray ring around the yolk: they were overcooked. Stick to 12 minutes off heat and go straight to the ice bath.
- Shells stick: your eggs were very fresh. Use eggs 1-2 weeks old, and peel under water.

**Swap:** Make them deviled-style: halve the eggs, mash the yolks with 1 tbsp plain Greek yogurt and 1 tsp mustard per 4 yolks, and spoon them back (eat within 3 days). Want fewer calories: eat 2 whole eggs plus 2 extra whites boiled the same way.
