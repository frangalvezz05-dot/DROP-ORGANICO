### S3 · Quick Red Enchilada Sauce
**Makes:** 5 portions of sauce (40 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separately · **Active:** 5 min · **Total:** 15 min
**Per portion:** 30 kcal · 1 g protein · 3 g carbs · 2 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat
**Cost/portion:** about $0.20 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Warm in a small pan or microwave 30-45 sec until steaming.
**Goes with:** M4, M11 (Mexican plates with S3)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 2 tsp olive oil (9 g)
- 2 tsp all-purpose flour (5 g)
- 1 Tbsp chili powder (8 g)
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin (0.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder (0.8 g)
- 1/4 tsp dried oregano (0.3 g)
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce (plain, canned) (122 g)
- 1/3 cup water (80 g)
- 1 tsp cider vinegar (5 g)
- 1/2 tsp fine salt (3 g)

**Steps**
1. Warm the oil in a small saucepan over medium heat until it shimmers, about 1 min.
2. Whisk in the flour, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and oregano. Cook 45-60 sec, whisking, until it smells toasty and looks like wet sand. Don't let it go dark.
3. Pour in the tomato sauce, water and vinegar while whisking. Scrape up any paste from the bottom.
4. Add the salt. Simmer on low 8-10 min, stirring now and then, until it coats the back of a spoon and the color deepens to brick red.
5. Taste. Add a pinch of salt if it tastes flat. Cool, then spoon into one jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 42 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Fire. One burner, 15 min, low and unattended after minute 5. Fits while the oven is heating.

**Fix it:**
- Bitter? The spices burned. Stir in 1/2 tsp honey and 1 tsp tomato sauce; if still bitter, start again with lower heat.
- Too thick when cold? Thin with 1-2 tsp water when you reheat.

**Swap:** Gluten-free: use 1 tsp cornstarch instead of the flour. Hotter: add 1/8 tsp cayenne.
