### FM5 · Turkey Sausage & Lentil Soup
**Makes:** 6 portions (~555 g each) · **Active:** 20 min · **Total:** 65 min
**Per portion:** 560 kcal · 28 g protein · 50 g carbs · 30 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** wheat
**Cost/portion:** about $2.70 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** 3 months · **Reheat:** Thaw overnight in the fridge (never on the counter). Reheat in a pot over medium 6-8 min, adding 1/4 cup water or broth per portion because the barley keeps drinking liquid, until steaming and 165 °F / 74 °C in the center. Microwave covered 3-4 min, stirring halfway.
**Goes with:** B6 Wraps & Tortillas (for dipping, toasted), V6 Chopped Cucumber-Tomato Salad, S9 Chimichurri (a spoonful on top)

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- fully cooked smoked turkey sausage, sliced into coins: 1 lb (454 g)
- olive oil: 1.5 tbsp (21 g)
- yellow onion, diced: 1 large (200 g)
- carrots, diced: 3 medium (200 g)
- celery, diced: 3 stalks (120 g)
- garlic, minced: 4 cloves (12 g)
- dried thyme: 1 tsp (2 g)
- brown or green lentils, dry, rinsed: 1.25 cups (250 g)
- diced tomatoes with their juice: 1 can (14.5 oz) (411 g)
- low-sodium chicken broth: 7 cups (1680 g)
- kale, stems removed, chopped: 3 cups packed (90 g)
- pearled barley: 1/4 cup (50 g)
- kosher salt: 1 tsp (5 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat 2 tsp of the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the sausage coins and cook 4-5 minutes until browned on both sides. Take them out and set aside.
2. Add the rest of the oil, onion, carrot and celery. Cook 6 minutes until the onion is soft and see-through. Add garlic and thyme for 30 seconds.
3. Add the lentils, barley, tomatoes with juice, broth and salt. Bring to a boil, lower to a steady simmer, and partly cover. Cook 30 minutes.
4. Add the sausage back and the kale. Simmer 10 more minutes. The soup is done when the lentils and barley are tender with no hard center and the kale is dark green and soft.
5. Taste and adjust the salt. The soup will thicken as it cools; that is normal. The soup must read 165 °F / 74 °C or more before you portion.
6. Cool fast: within 2 hours of cooking, ladle into shallow containers no deeper than 2 in / 5 cm. Leave uncovered about 20 minutes (or set the containers in an ice bath), then lid and refrigerate once the steam stops.
7. Portion and freeze: divide into 6 labeled containers (date on the lid), leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace, and freeze flat. Leave 1 in / 2.5 cm of headspace; soup expands. Freeze in zip bags laid flat if short on space.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop. Start at minute 10; simmers on its own from minute 25. Pairs with a long oven item like P2 or P7.

**Fix it:**
- Soup turned into stew overnight: the barley and lentils absorbed the broth. Add broth or water when reheating until it looks like soup again.
- Lentils split into mush: you cooked too hard. Keep a lazy simmer with small bubbles, not a rolling boil.

**Swap:** Swap the sausage for 1.5 cups of diced cooked chicken breast added in the last 5 minutes, or for a second can of chickpeas (vegetarian, check the broth). Gluten-free: leave out the barley and add 1/2 cup more lentils.
