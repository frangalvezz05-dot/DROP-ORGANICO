### V4 · Garlic Green Beans

**Makes:** 5 portions (about 120 g cooked each) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 25 min · **Oven:** 425 °F / 220 °C
**Per portion:** 100 kcal · 3 g protein · 11 g carbs · 6 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** none
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (texture) · **Reheat:** Eat cold in bowls, or microwave 45-60 sec.
**Goes with:** signature dishes A4, A6, A10, A11, D-5, C1, C4, C11, C12 · flavour families ASI, MED, COM

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- 1.5 lb, trimmed green beans (700 g)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
- 4 cloves, minced garlic cloves (12 g)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (4.5 g)
- 1/4 tsp black pepper (0.5 g)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (15 g)

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C.
2. Trim the ends off the green beans. Dry them well.
3. Toss with the oil, salt and pepper. Spread in one layer on a large pan.
4. Roast 10-12 minutes until they start to wrinkle.
5. Take out, scatter the garlic over, stir, and roast 4-6 minutes more. Done when the beans are wrinkled with brown spots and still have a little snap, and the garlic is pale gold, not dark.
6. Add the lemon juice. Portion into 5 containers (about 120 g each).

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Oven, minute 0 to 18 at 425 °F / 220 °C. The garlic goes on in the last 5 minutes.
**Fix it:**
- Bitter garlic: it burned. Always add it late, and take it out when pale gold.
- Limp beans: roasted too long or too low. Next time use the top rack and check at 12 minutes.

**Swap:** Use asparagus (cook 8-10 minutes) or snap peas (7-9 minutes). Frozen green beans work: roast from frozen for 20 minutes.
