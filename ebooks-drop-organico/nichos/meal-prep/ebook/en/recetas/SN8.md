### SN8 · Edamame Snack Boxes
**Makes:** 5 portions (about 90 g shelled edamame + 90 g veg + 14 g almonds) · **Active:** 10 min · **Total:** 20 min
**Per portion:** 220 kcal · 15 g protein · 15 g carbs · 13 g fat (USDA FoodData Central) · **Allergens:** soy, tree nuts, sesame, wheat
**Cost/portion:** about $1.65 (US average retail prices, USDA ERS (meat, eggs, dairy: Sep 2026; fruit and vegetables: 2023 data); your store will differ.)
**Fridge:** 4 days · **Freezer:** not recommended (see Reheat) · **Reheat:** Eat cold, or warm the edamame alone 30 seconds in the microwave. The assembled box is not freezer-friendly (vegetables go limp); plain cooked edamame freezes for 3 months.
**Goes with:** V5 Sesame Cabbage-Carrot Slaw, A2 and A7 plates (a spoonful on top); stands alone as a snack

**Ingredients** (US + metric)
- frozen shelled edamame: 1 bag (16 oz) (454 g)
- cherry tomatoes, halved: 2 cups (300 g)
- red bell pepper, cut in strips: 1 medium (150 g)
- dry-roasted almonds: 1/4 cup (70 g)
- toasted sesame oil: 1 tsp (5 g)
- low-sodium soy sauce: 2 tsp (12 g)
- flaky sea salt (pinch per box): 1/4 tsp (1 g)

**Steps**
1. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Add the frozen edamame and cook 4-5 minutes until they are bright green and tender with a little bite, and taste fully warm all the way through.
2. Drain and cool in a colander under cold water for 1 minute. Shake dry and pat with a towel. Wet edamame turn the box soggy.
3. Toss the edamame with the sesame oil, soy sauce and half the salt in a bowl.
4. Divide into 5 containers (about 90 g edamame each). Add the tomato halves and pepper strips (about 90 g of veg per box), and pack the almonds (14 g) in a small cup or corner so they stay crunchy.
5. Done when the edamame look glossy, not wet. Lid and chill right away. Eat within 4 days. Keep at 40 °F / 4 °C or colder.

**Sunday-sheet slot:** Stovetop, 5 minutes of active time. Boil the edamame while the oven items rest.

**Fix it:**
- Edamame tough and bland: they cooked too little or are old. Cook 2 minutes longer, and salt while they're still warm.
- Watery box: dry the edamame well and put the tomato in a separate cup if you pack it more than 2 days ahead.

**Swap:** Soy-free is not possible with edamame; swap for 1 1/2 cups of roasted chickpeas (SN5) per 5 boxes. Nut-free: swap the almonds for pumpkin seeds. Gluten-free: use tamari.
