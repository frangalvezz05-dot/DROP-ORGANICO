Three weeks in, you've followed three sheets. This week is half filled in. Everything is set except one sauce, and you choose it. The point isn't the sauce. It's learning how a choice moves through the sheet, the shopping list and the boxes, so that after this week you can plan your own.

**Who it's for:** you've done at least one plan week and you want to stop depending on the book.

**What comes out:** 10 boxes and 5 breakfasts. One protein is tofu, so this week is lighter on meat.

**Raw meat this week:** about 3.5 lb (1.6 kg) for one person: 3 lb pork shoulder after trimming (buy about 3¾ lb) and 8 oz ground turkey for the egg bites. Plus 2 lb of tofu.

## The Sunday Sheet · Week 4

**About 4½ hours before 0:00 (15 minutes):** oven to **300 °F / 150 °C**. Trim the pork. Rub it with the sugar and spices. Into a Dutch oven or deep dish with the vinegar and ½ cup water around it. Cover tightly. Roast 4-4½ hours. You're free until 0:00.

**Before 0:00 (about 10 minutes):** line 2 sheet pans with parchment. Grease 15 muffin cups. Get out an 8×8-inch (20 cm) dish with foil, the scale, thermometer, 10 boxes, 6 dressing cups, 10 sauce cups, 5 breakfast containers. Fill the kettle.

| Clock | Station | Do this | Done when | Recipe |

**Write your real time here:** start ______ finish ______ total ______ min (not counting the pork's 4½ hours alone in the oven).

*Why the pork goes in early:* at 300 °F it holds the oven for 4-4½ hours, and nothing else in this week cooks at 300 °F. Roasting it before the session frees the oven for the hot trays. Don't roast it on Saturday: that makes Thursday day 5.

**Allergens this week:** peanuts, soy, wheat, sesame, egg, milk, plus whatever your sauce adds. Many gochujang brands contain wheat. **If anyone eating this week has a peanut allergy, skip S5:** use S6 on A2 and A12 instead, and keep the peanut-ginger sauce out of the kitchen that day.

## The day-5 rule, applied

- **Friday = A7 + A12, frozen on Sunday.** Tofu, pork, rice, quinoa, gochujang sauce and peanut sauce freeze. The broccoli is safe frozen but softer. Re-crisp the tofu 6-8 min at 400 °F / 205 °C after thawing (the microwave makes it soft).
- **The raw slaw doesn't freeze.** That's why every slaw plate (A3, M5, A2) is Monday to Thursday. Dress it on the day.
- **If you chose S1 or S11:** they don't freeze, and they're fine here because M5 is on Tuesday and Thursday.
- **Thursday morning:** move Friday's boxes to the fridge.
