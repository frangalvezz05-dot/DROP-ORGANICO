This is the week for your first Sunday. Every decision is made. You shop from the list, cook from the sheet and fill the boxes.

**Who it's for:** you're new to batch cooking, or you tried it and got lost in five recipes at once. It uses one skillet, one small saucepan and the oven. No slow cooker, no blender.

**What comes out:** 10 boxes (lunch and dinner Monday to Friday for one person, or lunch for two) and 5 breakfasts.

**Raw meat this week:** 4 lb (1.8 kg) for one person: 2 lb chicken thighs and 2 lb ground turkey. That's the most of any week except Week 3. Week 2 drops to about 3.2 lb.

## The Sunday Sheet · Week 1

**Before 0:00 (about 10 minutes):** clear the counter. Line 4 sheet pans with parchment. Grease a 12-cup muffin tin plus 3 cups of a second tin. Get out the scale, thermometer, an 8×8-inch (20 cm) baking dish with foil, 10 boxes, 6 small sauce cups and 5 breakfast containers. Fill the kettle.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 0:00 | PREP ⏱ | Cut broccoli into florets with a flat side. Dry well. Toss with oil, salt, garlic powder on pan 1. | Florets dry to the touch | V1 |
| 0:05 | PREP ⏱ | Whisk the chicken marinade. Toss the thighs in it. Into the fridge. | Every piece coated | P1 |

**Write your real time here:** start ______ finish ______ total ______ min.

*Why this order:* the broccoli wants the hottest oven, so it goes first. Everything at 425 °F shares the next 50 minutes. The egg bites need the coolest oven, so they go last while you pack. The recipe cards say B1 cooks at 375 °F; at 425 °F it fits beside another tray if the foil is sealed tight, so check it at 25 minutes.

## The day-5 rule, applied

- Sunday is day 0. Friday is day 5, outside the 3-4 days USDA FSIS gives cooked leftovers in the fridge.
- **Friday = A1 ×2, frozen on Sunday.** Chicken, rice and teriyaki all freeze well. The broccoli is safe frozen but comes out softer (that's why the storage chart says "not recommended" for texture). Re-crisp it 5 minutes in a hot oven or air fryer if you mind.
- **Thursday morning:** move both A1 boxes to the fridge. They thaw by Friday.
- No salmon this week, so no day-1-2 rule.
- S1 (chipotle-lime yogurt) doesn't freeze, and it's only on Monday-Thursday plates. That's why it isn't on Friday.
