The rules (USDA FSIS)
  1. Fridge at 40 °F (4 °C) or colder. Cooked leftovers keep 3-4 days. Count from the day you cook (day 0).
  2. The day-5 rule (this book): anything you'll eat on day 5 or later goes in the freezer on cooking day. Move it to the fridge the morning before.
  3. 2 hours max at room temperature after cooking, 1 hour if the room is above 90 °F (32 °C). Spread food shallow so it cools fast.
  4. Freezer at 0 °F (-18 °C). Frozen food stays safe as long as it stays frozen. The months below are for quality (taste and texture), not safety.
  5. Thaw in the fridge, never on the counter. Or on defrost in the microwave, then reheat and eat right away.
  6. Reheat leftovers to 165 °F (74 °C). Cover, stir halfway, check the thickest piece.
  7. When in doubt, throw it out. If it smells off or you don't know how old it is.
