If every recipe sends you out for a new jar, your weekly list gets long and your cupboard fills with half-used bottles.

The 60 recipes in this book share a small pantry. Buy it once. After that, the weekly shopping list is mostly fresh food: meat, vegetables, yogurt, herbs, lemons and limes.

**Store them well.** Opened chipotles in adobo keep longer if you freeze the rest flat in a zip-top bag and break off a piece when needed. Natural peanut butter and tahini separate on the shelf. Stir them well once, then keep them in the fridge if the label says so.

## On the weekly list, not in the pantry

These spoil or get used up fast, so they're on each week's shopping list: garlic, fresh ginger, lemons, limes, cilantro, parsley, plain Greek yogurt, eggs, tortillas, pasta, potatoes, sweet potatoes and all meat, fish and tofu.

## The gear: 6 things

You probably own most of them.

| ✓ | Tool | Why you need it |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **2 rimmed sheet pans** (half-sheet, about 18 × 13 in / 46 × 33 cm) | Two trays roast at the same time. Rims stop juices from dripping. |
| ☐ | **9 × 13 in (23 × 33 cm) baking dish** | Oven rice and bakes. |
| ☐ | **Large skillet**, about 12 in (30 cm) | Ground meat and sauces. Big enough not to crowd. |
| ☐ | **Pot with a lid**, 4 qt (4 L) or more | Quinoa, pasta, lentils, soups. |
| ☐ | **Digital kitchen scale** with a tare button, in grams | Equal boxes (Chapter 3). |
| ☐ | **Instant-read thermometer** | Safe temperatures (Chapters 2 and 4). The one tool you shouldn't skip. |

**Plus containers.** 10 meal containers and 5 small sauce cups for the ×1 week. Double for ×2. Check the bottom or the box: they should say microwave-safe and freezer-safe. Two-compartment boxes help keep sauce and raw parts apart.

**Small stuff:** parchment paper, foil, masking tape and a marker for labels, a fridge thermometer.

**Nice to have, not needed:** a slow cooker (Week 2 uses one; the recipe gives an oven option), an air fryer (best for reheating potatoes and tofu), a sharp chef's knife if yours is dull.

## One rule that keeps the pantry stocked

**When you open the last one, write it on next week's list.** Not when it runs out. When you open it. A taped note on the inside of the cupboard door works.
