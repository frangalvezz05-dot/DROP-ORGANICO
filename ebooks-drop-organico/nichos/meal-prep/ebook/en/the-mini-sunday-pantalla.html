The Mini Sunday

One protein, one base, one vegetable, three sauces. Three different lunches. Five boxes.

A free sample of The Sunday Sheet.

"…the same ingredients precooked and rearranged in slightly different ways got really old really fast."

— reader review

If you've tried meal prep, you know the Wednesday feeling. Same chicken, same rice, third day in a row.

This sample shows the fix in one short Sunday session. You cook one tray of chicken, one dish of rice and one tray of broccoli. Then three sauces turn them into three different lunches. Monday to Friday, five boxes, and nothing tastes like the day before.

Three notes before you cook

  • Not medical or nutrition advice. Calories and protein are calculated from USDA data. This sample doesn't tell you how much to eat. If you have a medical condition or follow a diet set by a professional, talk to a registered dietitian or your doctor.
  • Allergens. These recipes contain milk, soy, wheat and sesame (marked on each plate and recipe). Soy sauce usually contains wheat. Brands differ: read the label of what you buy.
  • Food safety. Storage times and temperatures follow USDA FSIS. Use a thermometer for the chicken.

Part 1

The method on one page

Every box = 1 protein + 1 base + 1 vegetable + 1 sauce.

The protein, base and vegetable are cooked once, on Sunday. The sauce changes the plate. One tray of lemon-garlic chicken becomes a chipotle bowl on Monday, a teriyaki bowl on Tuesday and a tahini bowl on Wednesday.

The sauce always lives in its own small cup. It goes on after you reheat. Rice that sits in sauce for four days turns soggy; rice that meets its sauce at lunch doesn't.

The day-5 rule. USDA FSIS says cooked leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge. Count from Sunday:

Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri
cook (day 0) day 1 day 2 day 3 day 4 day 5

Friday is day 5, outside the window. So Friday's box goes in the freezer on Sunday. On Thursday morning, move it to the fridge. It's thawed by lunch on Friday.

The recipes

Protein

P1

Lemon-Garlic Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs

Makes 5 portions (about 136 g cooked each)Active 10 minTotal 40 minOven 425 °F / 220 °C
280kcal
36 gprotein
2 gcarbs
13 gfat
PER PORTION · USDA FoodData Central
Allergensnone
Fridge4 days
Freezer3 months
ReheatMicrowave 60-90 sec, covered, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).

Ingredients (US + metric)

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (907 g)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice (45 g)
  • 4 cloves, minced garlic cloves (12 g)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano (1.5 g)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (2.5 g)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (1 g)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425 °F / 220 °C. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Whisk the oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper in a large bowl. Add the thighs and toss until every piece is coated. Leave 10 minutes, or up to 8 hours in the fridge.
  3. Spread the thighs in one layer with space between them. Do not stack.
  4. Roast 22-28 minutes. Done when the edges are browned, the juices run clear and a thermometer in the thickest piece reads 165 °F / 74 °C.
  5. Rest 5 minutes on the pan. Slice into strips or bite-size pieces.
  6. Portion into 5 containers (about 135 g each). Cool for up to 1 hour at room temperature, then refrigerate.

Sunday-sheet slot

Oven, minute 0. Goes in first, comes out at minute 25-28, then rests while you fill the oven with the next tray.

Fix it

  • Pale and wet instead of browned: the pan was crowded or the oven was cool. Put it back 5 minutes under the broiler, watching it closely.
  • Dry? Thighs forgive, but if they went past 185 °F, toss the slices with a spoon of the pan juices before storing.

Swap

Use bone-in thighs (about 2.75 lb, add 10 minutes) or chicken breast cut into 1-inch strips (cook 15-18 minutes). For no lemon, use 1 tbsp red wine vinegar.

Hands-Off Oven Rice

Makes 5 portions (about 160 g cooked each)Active 5 minTotal 45 minOven 375 °F / 190 °C
230kcal
4 gprotein
44 gcarbs
3 gfat
PER PORTION · USDA FoodData Central
Allergensnone
Fridge4 days
Freezer2 months
ReheatMicrowave 90 sec with 1 tbsp of water, covered, until steaming hot (165 °F / 74 °C).

Ingredients (US + metric)

  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice (278 g)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (14 g)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375 °F / 190 °C. Boil 2 1/4 cups of water in a kettle or pot.
  2. Rinse the rice in a sieve under cold water for 30 seconds. Drain well.
  3. Put the rice, oil and salt in an 8x8-inch / 20 cm baking dish (or any dish with a tight lid). Pour in the boiling water and stir once.
  4. Cover tightly with foil, crimping all the edges. Bake 25-30 minutes without opening it.
  5. Done when all the water is absorbed and small steam holes show on the surface. Leave covered 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  6. Spread the rice out in a thin layer to cool, then portion into 5 containers (160 g each) within 1 hour of cooking. Refrigerate right away.

Sunday-sheet slot

Oven, minute 0 to 30, then rest 10 minutes off. Fits next to a 425 °F tray if you cover it well; or cook it before the high-heat trays.

Fix it

  • Crunchy grains: the foil leaked steam. Add 2 tbsp hot water, re-cover and give it 10 minutes.
  • Gummy: too much water or no rinse. Spread on a pan and dry 5 minutes in the oven.

Swap

Brown rice: use 1 1/2 cups rice and 2 3/4 cups boiling water, bake 55-60 minutes. Cauliflower rice is not a swap in this recipe (it does not bake like rice).

110kcal
5 gprotein
12 gcarbs
6 gfat
PER PORTION · USDA FoodData Central
Allergensnone
Fridge4 days
Freezernot recommended (texture)
ReheatEat cold in bowls, or 10 minutes in a hot oven or air fryer. Microwave makes it soft but it is safe.

Ingredients (US + metric)

  • 2 large crowns (about 2 lb), cut into florets broccoli crowns (900 g)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (27 g)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (table-salt equivalent) (6 g)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder (3 g)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 450 °F / 230 °C.
  2. Cut the broccoli into bite-size florets, with a flat cut side if possible. Cut thick stems into coins.
  3. Dry well. Toss with the oil, salt and garlic powder.
  4. Spread over a large pan with space between pieces. Place cut sides down.
  5. Roast 18-22 minutes without stirring more than once. Done when the tips are blackened in spots, the stems pierce with a knife, and the florets look dark green and slightly shrunken.
  6. Portion into 5 containers (about 130 g each).

Sunday-sheet slot

Oven, minute 0 to 22 at 450 °F / 230 °C. Hottest tray of the day. Do it first, then lower the oven for the rest.

Fix it

  • Limp and olive-colored: too wet, too crowded or too low an oven. Dry it and use 2 pans.
  • Bitter, burnt tips: oven too hot or pan too close to the top. Lower the rack.

Swap

Use cauliflower (same time) or Brussels sprouts halved (add 5 minutes). Frozen broccoli: roast from frozen at 450 °F for 25 minutes, less crisp.

Sauce

S1

Chipotle-Lime Yogurt

Makes 5 portions of sauce (40 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separatelyActive 5 minTotal 5 min
30kcal
4 gprotein
2 gcarbs
1 gfat
PER PORTION · USDA FoodData Central
Allergensmilk
Fridge4 days
Freezernot recommended (texture)
ReheatServe cold. Don't heat it. Stir before using.

Macro note: Chipotle in adobo is costed with the closest USDA entry (canned hot red chili peppers); brands vary a little.

Ingredients (US + metric)

  • 3/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt (170 g)
  • 1 pepper canned chipotle pepper in adobo, minced (+ 1 tsp of the adobo sauce) (15 g)
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice (15 g)
  • 1 small clove garlic, grated (3 g)
  • 1/4 tsp ground cumin (0.5 g)
  • 1 tsp olive oil (4.5 g)
  • 1/8 tsp fine salt (0.7 g)

Steps

  1. Mince the chipotle pepper as fine as you can. Grate the garlic.
  2. Spoon the yogurt into a bowl or a 1-cup jar.
  3. Add the chipotle, 1 tsp adobo sauce, lime juice, garlic, cumin, oil and salt. Stir until the color is an even pale orange with no white streaks.
  4. Taste. Add a pinch more salt if it tastes flat, or a little more adobo if you want heat.
  5. Scoop into one small lidded container or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 40 g each). Chill at least 30 min so the garlic and chipotle settle in.

Sunday-sheet slot

Cold. Make it in the 5-minute gap while the oven preheats or the chicken rests. No burner, no oven.

Fix it

  • Too thin? Your yogurt was watery. Stir in 1 more Tbsp of yogurt, or drain it in a sieve for 10 min next time.
  • Too hot the next day? Heat builds as it sits. Stir in 1 Tbsp yogurt to calm it.

Swap

No dairy: use unsweetened plain coconut or soy yogurt (macros change). Less heat: use half the chipotle.

S4

Lighter Teriyaki

Makes 5 portions of sauce (35 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separatelyActive 5 minTotal 12 min
40kcal
1 gprotein
7 gcarbs
0 gfat
PER PORTION · USDA FoodData Central
Allergenssoy, wheat, sesame
Fridge4 days
Freezer3 months
ReheatWarm in a pan or microwave 30 sec. Stir; it thickens as it cools.

Ingredients (US + metric)

  • 1/4 cup reduced-sodium soy sauce (60 g)
  • 1/4 cup water (60 g)
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp honey (32 g)
  • 1 Tbsp rice vinegar or cider vinegar (15 g)
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely grated (2 g)
  • 1 clove garlic, grated (3 g)
  • 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (2 g)
  • 2 tsp cornstarch (5 g)
  • 1 Tbsp (for the cornstarch) water (15 g)

Steps

  1. Whisk the cornstarch with 1 Tbsp cold water in a small cup until smooth. Set aside.
  2. In a small saucepan, combine soy sauce, 1/4 cup water, honey, vinegar, ginger and garlic. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring.
  3. Give the cornstarch cup another stir, then pour it in while whisking. Simmer 1-2 min until the sauce turns glossy and coats the back of a spoon.
  4. Remove from the heat and stir in the sesame oil.
  5. Cool 10 min. Pour into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 34 g each).

Sunday-sheet slot

Fire. One burner, 10 min. Easy to run while the oven is on.

Fix it

  • Lumpy? The cornstarch wasn't mixed smooth. Pour through a small sieve.
  • Too thin? Simmer 1 more min. Too thick when cold? Whisk in 1 tsp warm water.

Swap

Gluten-free and soy-free: use coconut aminos and the same amount of honey, and taste for salt. Less sweet: use 1 Tbsp honey.

Lemon-Tahini Sauce

Makes 5 portions of sauce (35 g each, about 2-3 Tbsp), stored separatelyActive 5 minTotal 5 min
70kcal
2 gprotein
3 gcarbs
6 gfat
PER PORTION · USDA FoodData Central
Allergenssesame
Fridge4 days
Freezer2 months
ReheatServe cool. It sets firm in the fridge; whisk in 1 Tbsp water per portion.

Ingredients (US + metric)

  • 1/4 cup tahini (stir the jar first) (60 g)
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice (45 g)
  • cold, 1/4 cup water (60 g)
  • 1 small clove garlic, grated (3 g)
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt (1.5 g)
  • a pinch ground cumin (0.3 g)

Steps

  1. Put the tahini in a bowl. Add the lemon juice, garlic, salt and cumin. Whisk. It will seize into a thick, pale paste. That's normal.
  2. Whisk in the cold water 1 Tbsp at a time. The paste loosens and turns creamy and light.
  3. Stop when it falls from the whisk in a thick ribbon.
  4. Taste. More lemon if it's heavy, more salt if it's flat. Spoon into a jar or 5 mini containers (about 2-3 Tbsp / 34 g each).

Sunday-sheet slot

Cold. 5 minutes anywhere. No heat.

Fix it

  • Bitter? Cheaper tahini can be. Add 1 tsp honey and 1 tsp lemon.
  • Grainy or split? Whisk in 1 tsp cold water at a time until smooth again.

Swap

Sesame allergy: use sunflower seed butter (macros change). Sweeter: add 1 tsp honey.

At lunch: take the sauce cup out. Microwave the box loosely covered, stir halfway, until steaming. Sauce on top, cold. A squeeze of lime or lemon wakes it up.

The rules (USDA FSIS): fridge at 40 °F / 4 °C or colder. Into the fridge within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour if the room is above 90 °F). Thaw in the fridge, never on the counter. Reheat to 165 °F / 74 °C. When in doubt, throw it out.
