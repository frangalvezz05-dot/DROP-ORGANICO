A signature plate is one protein, one base, one vegetable and one sauce that we put together on purpose. There are 48, 12 per flavor family. Each one tells you what it adds up to and how to pack it.

Use this chapter two ways:
- **To swap a plate in a plan week.** Same parts, different sauce? Find it here and check its numbers.
- **To plan your own week.** Pick 5 plates that share 2 proteins, 2 bases and 2 vegetables. Then cook those parts on Sunday.

## How to read a plate

- **kcal and protein** are for one box built from **one standard portion** of each part, the portion on its recipe card. Calculated from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, including the sauce. Kcal rounded to 10, protein to 1 g. In the plan weeks, some batches are shared by more or fewer boxes, so the week chapters show the numbers for those boxes.
- **Allergens** are the 9 major US allergens (FDA) found in any of the 4 parts. Brands differ: read your labels.
- **Fridge** is the shortest fridge life of the 4 parts. Count from Sunday (day 0).
- **Freezer** tells you if the box can go in the freezer for Friday (the day-5 rule, Chapter 4), and which part comes out softer if it does. Safe, just a different texture.
- **The 3 lines** are how you pack and eat it: (1) what goes in the box, (2) what stays apart, (3) how to reheat and finish. The finish (lime, lemon, pepper, scallion) isn't counted in the numbers. It adds almost nothing and wakes up day-4 food.
- Grams are the standard portion. Weigh the batch and divide it (Chapter 3); the scale wins.

## Mexican-style (MEX) · 12 plates

Chipotle, lime, salsa verde, red enchilada. The fridge-friendliest family: most of these sauces freeze, and the yogurt one is never heated.

### M1 · Chipotle Lemon Chicken Fajita Bowl
P1 Lemon-Garlic Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs + B1 Hands-Off Oven Rice + V2 Sheet-Pan Fajita Peppers & Onions + S1 Chipotle-Lime Yogurt  
**640 kcal · 46 g protein** · Allergens: milk · Fridge: 4 days · Freezer: yes, except S1 (texture)
1. Box: 160 g rice, 130 g fajita peppers & onions, 136 g lemon-garlic chicken on top.
2. Apart: 40 g Chipotle-Lime Yogurt.
3. Microwave covered, stir or turn halfway, until steaming hot: 165 °F / 74 °C. Then sauce cold on top, a squeeze of lime.

### Four checks before you commit to a combo

1. **Fridge days.** The box keeps as long as its shortest part. Salmon (P8), zucchini (V3) and the cucumber-tomato salad (V6) are 3 days; most parts are 4.
2. **Friday.** If the combo is your Friday box, every hot part must freeze. Raw vegetables (V5, V6) and yogurt sauces (S1, S7, S11) don't.
3. **Allergens.** Add up the allergens of the four parts. Their recipe cards list them, and so does Chapter 21.
4. **The oven.** Two oven proteins at very different temperatures (P7 at 300 °F, everything else at 400-450 °F) don't share a session easily. Week 4 shows the workaround.
