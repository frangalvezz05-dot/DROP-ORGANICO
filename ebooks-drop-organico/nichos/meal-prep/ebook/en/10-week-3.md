# 10 · Week 3 · The Freezer-Ahead Week

> "Keep em in the freezer and you're good for weeks."
> — reader review

<!-- Frase F1 (frases.md), fragmento literal de una reseña de comprador (The Big Bean, Gumroad): https://soberfitness.gumroad.com/l/thebigbean -->

This week you cook a little more than you eat. The meatballs are made twice. One batch feeds this week. The other goes into the freezer as a reserve for a week when Sunday doesn't happen.

**Who it's for:** you've done two weeks and you know a busy week is coming. Or you want a stock in the freezer before you need it.

**What comes out:** 10 boxes, 5 breakfast burritos, 1 salmon dinner for Sunday night, and **4 meatball portions (24 meatballs) for the freezer**.

**Raw meat and fish this week:** 6 lb (2.7 kg) for one person: 4 lb ground turkey and 2 lb salmon. It's the biggest shop of the plan because a third of it is the freezer reserve.

## The Sunday Sheet · Week 3

**Before 0:00 (about 10 minutes):** line 5 sheet pans with parchment. Fill a large pot with 3 quarts (3 L) of water. Get out the scale, thermometer, 10 boxes, 9 sauce cups, a freezer bag for the meatballs, foil or parchment for 5 burritos.

*Why this order:* vegetables roast hottest, so they go first. The oven drops 25 degrees for the meatballs and stays there for the salmon. The stove runs the whole session on two burners; the oven never waits for it.

## The day-5 rule, applied

- **Salmon on days 1-2.** All 4 salmon boxes are Monday and Tuesday. The 5th piece is Sunday dinner. Salmon is safe for 3 days, but it dries and smells stronger as the days pass.
- **Zucchini (V3) keeps 3 days.** The zucchini plates are D-3 (Mon-Tue) and C3 (Wed).
- **Friday = A10 + D-5, frozen on Sunday.** Meatballs, pasta, quinoa, teriyaki and chimichurri all freeze. The green beans are safe frozen but come out softer.
- **Friday's burrito** goes in the freezer too. Burritos keep 4 days in the fridge.
- **Thursday morning:** move both Friday boxes and the burrito to the fridge.
- **The reserve:** 24 meatballs, labeled with today's date. Best within 3 months. Reheat from frozen: 12 min at 350 °F / 175 °C, until 165 °F / 74 °C in the center. Pair them with any base and sauce from the Mix & Match chart (Chapter 16).
