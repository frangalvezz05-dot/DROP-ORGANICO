# 9 · Week 2 · The Budget Week

> "Simply because it gets expensive and hard to source all the meat sometimes... especially the more expensive cuts."

This week spends less on meat without cutting the protein. Two tricks do it. Lentils stretch 1 lb of ground beef into 5 portions. Chicken breast goes in the slow cooker with a jar of salsa and needs nothing from you for hours.

**Who it's for:** you did Week 1 and want a cheaper week, or meat prices are the reason you stopped prepping.

**What comes out:** 10 boxes and 5 jars of overnight oats.

**Raw meat this week:** about 3.2 lb (1.45 kg) for one person: 1 lb ground beef and 2.2 lb chicken breast. That's 0.8 lb less than Week 1.

**No slow cooker?** The P2 card doesn't have a tested oven method yet [SIN DATO]. Use P3 Never-Dry Baked Chicken Breast instead: same chicken, same weight. It roasts at 425 °F, so it shares the oven with this week's trays. Shred or slice it and stir in 2 Tbsp of jarred salsa per box. The numbers in the menu will change a little.

## The Sunday Sheet · Week 2

**2½ hours before 0:00 (10 minutes):** chicken breasts in the slow cooker in one layer. Cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and salt on top. Pour the salsa over. No water. Lid on, **HIGH**. Now go do something else.

**Before 0:00 (about 10 minutes):** line 2 sheet pans with parchment, keep a third pan bare for the potatoes. Get out the scale, thermometer, a large pot, 2 small saucepans, blender, 10 boxes, 10 sauce cups, 5 jars of at least 12 oz (350 ml).

| Clock | Station | Do this | Done when | Recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | OVEN | Heat to **425 °F / 220 °C** with the bare pan inside. | Oven signals hot | B3 |
| 0:00 | STOVE | Large pot, medium-high: oil, then the beef. Break it up. 6-8 min. | No pink. **160 °F / 71 °C** | P5 |
| 0:00 | PREP ⏱ | Halve the potatoes (quarter big ones). Dry. Toss with oil and spices. Slice peppers and onions ½ inch. Toss with oil and spices on a lined pan. | Same-size pieces | B3, V2 |
| 0:10 | OVEN | **Potatoes onto the hot pan**, cut side down (lower rack). Timer: 20 min, then flip. **Peppers in** (top rack). Timer: stir at 15 min. | They sizzle | B3, V2 |
| 0:10 | STOVE ⏱ | Pot: onion + carrot 5 min, garlic + tomato paste + spices 1 min, then rinsed lentils, salt, 3 cups water. Boil, then simmer partly covered 22-28 min. Stir now and then. | — | P5 |
| 0:15 | STOVE ⏱ | Saucepan 1: whisk all the BBQ ingredients, bring to a gentle bubble, then low 10-12 min. | Spoon leaves a clear trail | S10 |
| 0:25 | OVEN | Stir the peppers. | — | V2 |
| 0:30 | OVEN | Flip the potatoes. Timer: 15 min. | — | B3 |
| 0:30 | PREP ⏱ | Cut zucchini in ½-inch half-moons, red onion in wedges. Toss with oil and spices on a lined pan. | — | V3 |
| 0:35 | STOVE ⏱ | Saucepan 2: oil, flour + spices 45-60 sec, then tomato sauce, water, vinegar, salt. Low 8-10 min. | Coats a spoon, brick red | S3 |
| 0:38 | OVEN | **Peppers out** (25-28 min). Lime on top. **Zucchini in.** Timer: turn at 12 min. | Onions soft and golden at the edges | V2, V3 |
| 0:45-0:50 | OVEN | **Potatoes out.** | Deep golden cut sides, knife slides in | B3 |
| 0:45 | STOVE | Check the beef and lentils. Add ¼ cup water at a time if it dries too soon. Off the heat when done. | Lentils tender, pot thick like chili | P5 |
| 0:50 | SLOW COOKER | Check the chicken. Shred with 2 forks. Stir in lime juice and ½ cup of the cooking liquid. If it isn't ready, check again at 1:10. | **165 °F / 74 °C**, shreds with no effort | P2 |
| 0:55 | COLD ⏱ | Overnight oats: dry mix, then yogurt, milk, honey, vanilla. Into 5 jars, berries on top. Fridge. | No dry pockets | D3 |
| 0:58-1:03 | OVEN | **Zucchini out** (20-25 min). Lemon juice on top. Turn on the **broiler, high**. | Golden edges, tender middle | V3 |
| 1:05 | OVEN | Tomatillos, jalapeño, onion, unpeeled garlic on a foil-lined pan under the broiler, 5-7 min. Flip, 4-5 min more. | Blistered, soft, slumped | S2 |
| 1:05 | ASSEMBLY ⏱ | Weigh each batch, divide by its box count (table below). Fill the boxes. Stack 6 tortillas with parchment between them, into a zip bag. | — | all |
| 1:20 | COLD | Cool the broiled salsa 5 min. Slip the garlic out of its skin. Blend with cilantro, lime, salt. | Bright, not flat | S2 |
| 1:30 | ASSEMBLY | Sauce into cups. Lids, labels. Fridge for Mon-Thu, **freezer for Friday's 2 M11 boxes**. The tortilla bag stays in the fridge. | Fridge 40 °F / 4 °C or below | all |

**Write your real time here:** start ______ finish ______ total ______ min.

*Why this order:* one oven temperature all session, so nothing waits for the oven to change. The broiler goes last because it needs the whole top of the oven. The lentil pot simmers on its own in the middle of the session.

## The day-5 rule, applied

- **Friday = M11 ×2, frozen on Sunday.** Shredded chicken, peppers and enchilada sauce all freeze (3 months). Freeze the filling and the sauce cup. Tortillas keep 7 days in the fridge, so they stay in their bag.
- **Thursday morning:** move both M11 boxes to the fridge.
- **Zucchini (V3) keeps 3 days, not 4.** Both zucchini plates (M4, M9) are eaten by Wednesday.
- No salmon this week.
