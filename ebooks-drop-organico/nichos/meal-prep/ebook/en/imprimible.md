## Page 1 · How to use this pack

Print what you need, not all of it.

- **Every week:** 1 Sunday Sheet + 1 shopping list + 1 sheet of box labels.
- **Once:** the storage chart (cupboard door), the fridge cards (fridge door), the Mix & Match poster.
- **When you plan your own week:** a blank Sunday Sheet and Build Your Plate.

Calories and protein come from USDA FoodData Central. Storage times follow USDA FSIS. This pack isn't medical or nutrition advice. Allergens are listed for the recipes as written; read the labels of what you buy.

## Pages 20-22 · Fridge cards

Half-page cards, 2 per sheet, for the fridge door.

### Card 1 · The day-5 rule

**Cooked food keeps 3-4 days in the fridge (USDA FSIS).**

| Sun | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cook · day 0 | day 1 | day 2 | day 3 | day 4 | **day 5 → freezer on Sunday** |

- ❄ Friday's boxes go in the freezer on **Sunday**.
- **Thursday morning:** move them to the fridge.
- Salmon: Monday and Tuesday only.
- 3-day parts: salmon, zucchini, cucumber-tomato salad. Eat them by Wednesday.
- Fridge 40 °F / 4 °C or colder. Freezer 0 °F / -18 °C.
- When in doubt, throw it out.

### Card 2 · Reheating by part

| Part | How |
|---|---|
| Chicken, turkey, beef, pork, meatballs | Covered, 1 Tbsp water or juices, until 165 °F / 74 °C. Chicken breast at 70% power. |
| Salmon | Cold, or 50% power 60-90 sec. Don't boil it. |
| Tofu, potatoes, chickpeas | Oven or air fryer 400 °F / 205 °C, 5-10 min. Microwave makes them soft. |
| Rice, quinoa, pasta | 1 Tbsp water on top, covered, 60-90 sec. |
| Roasted vegetables | Cold, or microwave 45-60 sec. Hot pan for better texture. |
| Slaw, cucumber-tomato salad | Never heated. Dress on the day. |
| Yogurt sauces (chipotle, tzatziki, honey-mustard) | Never heated. Cold, on top. |
| Other sauces | Warm 20-30 sec, or cool. Stir; thin with 1 tsp water if set. |
| Frozen box | Thawed in the fridge overnight. Forgot? Defrost setting, then reheat and eat right away. |

**Always:** cover loosely, stir halfway, check the thickest piece: 165 °F / 74 °C.
